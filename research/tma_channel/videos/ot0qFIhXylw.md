## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings ([1:52]-[2:58]) — three free indicators:
  1. **"3 Smoothed Moving Averages" by Hamit Safi** (search "smooth" in the
     TradingView indicator list). Set the three to **21, 50 and 200 SMMA**.
     Styling: 21 = white, 50 = green, 200 = red, all full colour, second thickness.
  2. **RSI (relative strength index)** — length not stated, so **default 14
     implied**. Colour yellow. **Untick the upper band and the lower band; set the
     middle band to 50 as a solid line; remove the background.** So the only RSI
     level used is **50**.
  3. **Engulfing candle indicator by "reminos"** — **leave all settings factory**.
  4. Later he temporarily adds a **10 SMMA** to illustrate parabolic moves, then
     says "you don't need that small of a moving average, you can just keep it to
     these three: the 21, the 50 and the 200" ([9:31]-[9:52]).
- Setup/context required — the primary trend filter ([3:00]-[3:35]):
  - **Price above the 200 SMMA → longs only.**
  - **Price below the 200 SMMA → shorts only.**
  - Additional confirmation that a trend is real: **the moving averages line up in
    order (21, then 50, then 200) / "fanning"** after a 200-cross
    ([3:48]-[3:58], [10:40]-[10:44]).
  - **Do not trade consolidation.** Definition given twice: "while the price is
    playing in between moving averages it's considered consolidation"
    ([4:44]-[5:04]) and "the moving averages are weaving in and out of each other —
    do not trade this type of market" ([7:50]-[8:03]). Also [10:35]-[10:40].
- Entry trigger (trend-following leg) ([3:58]-[4:47]):
  - Wait for a **rejection off any one of the three moving averages** — in practice
    the **21 SMMA retest** is the one he keeps pointing to.
  - The entry candle must be a **"big fat momentum move candle"**: "you do not need
    fully engulfing candles ... but what you want to trade off of is big fat
    candles. It doesn't have to engulf the previous candle but it has to be a
    strong big fat momentum move." The engulfing indicator is **optional help**,
    not a requirement.
  - He counted **five such entries** off one 6-day ETHUSD downswing.
- Entry trigger (reversal / trend-change leg) ([6:55]-[9:03]):
  - When price crosses back over the moving averages, **start looking for RSI
    divergence**.
  - **Regular bullish divergence:** price makes lower lows while **RSI makes higher
    lows** → possible reversal ([6:58]-[7:10]).
  - Confirmation stack: "**right as the RSI is trading above the 50 level** you see
    this divergence breaking the previous trend line ... with strong consecutive
    bullish candles" ([7:10]-[7:23]).
  - **Hidden bullish divergence:** price makes a **low then a higher low** while
    **RSI makes a low then a lower low** → "the price is higher but it's even more
    oversold, meaning the price should go up" ([8:20]-[8:49]).
  - During a suspected reversal, **only take very short-term scalps: enter as price
    crosses a moving average, target the next moving average** ([7:28]-[7:50],
    restated [8:57]-[9:01]).
- Exit / take profit — he gives three options and refuses to pick one:
  1. **Fixed pip target with lot size adjusted to hit the desired dollar profit** —
     his preference: "if I can get 10 pips off a move I'm happy because I know how
     to adjust my lot size ... whether it's 10 pips or 15 pips or 5 pips"
     ([5:44]-[6:28]). Keep **the same position size and same take profit** each time.
  2. **Trailing stop for the big swing** — "keep a trailing stop loss **but not too
     close**; if you would have kept your trailing stop on the 21 you would have
     gotten stopped out one two three times" ([6:06]-[6:20]). So: **do not trail on
     the 21 SMMA.**
  3. **Support/resistance targets**, from the 1h markup ([12:02]-[12:29]).
  - Explicit exit signal for longs: **every time a higher high forms, look for
    (regular bearish) divergence — price higher highs vs RSI lower highs — these
    are exit points for your long** ([10:11]-[10:35]).
- Stop loss: only given once, inside the worked S/R example ([12:29]-[12:49]) —
  after the hidden divergence + engulfing candle, "put your short position in here
  right after you got that engulfing candle, targeting the **stop loss right above
  it**, and your take profit here at the bottom at this level that was previously
  known as support." So: **stop just above the entry candle / swing high; target
  the prior S/R level.**
- Filters he adds:
  - **Session:** "I do highly suggest however that you're trading either **London
    session or New York session** because **Asian session does not move — you're
    getting a long period of consolidation 90% of the time**" ([11:36]-[11:49]).
    No clock times or timezone given.
  - **Never trade consolidation** (see above) — the one market condition he
    excludes outright, stated in the video's first 10 seconds.
  - **1h S/R overlay** to define target zones, then drop back to 15m
    ([12:02]-[12:29]).

## Vague / untestable / chart-pointed claims
- [4:07]-[4:47] The whole entry sequence is narrated over the chart: "we had a
  rejection here off the 21 ... we had one right here we had another one right
  here". **"Big fat momentum move candle" is never quantified** — no ATR multiple,
  no body-size ratio, no minimum range. This is the single biggest gap in an
  otherwise mechanical system.
- [3:48]-[3:58] "all of the moving averages start lining up in order" — no rule for
  how long they must stay ordered, or how much separation counts.
- [4:44]-[5:04] "these types of areas right here you want to avoid" — consolidation
  is defined by eye ("price playing in between moving averages"), not numerically.
- [5:04]-[5:10] "if you only have this much chart to look at" — lookback length for
  judging trend never specified.
- [7:10]-[7:19] "breaking the previous trend line right there" — trend line is drawn
  discretionarily; no anchor rule.
- [8:14]-[8:25] "we have a low and a higher low right there, that is your new trend
  line" — swing-point selection is by eye, so both the divergence and the structure
  reading depend on unstated pivot definitions.
- [9:22]-[9:31] "this one right here bounced off of the 10 period moving average" —
  he adds then discards the 10 SMMA; ambiguous whether parabolic moves should use it.
- [5:33]-[5:44] "how long should I hold these trades for ... that's depending on
  you" — he **explicitly declines** to give a holding rule.
- [11:12]-[11:36] "this is going to teach you how to use all of my strategies
  combined into one" — the video is a synthesis pointer to ~60 other videos; several
  components (S/R drawing, trend lines) are only referenced, not taught here.
