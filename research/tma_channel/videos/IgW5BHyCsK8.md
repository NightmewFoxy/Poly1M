# VWAP vs Anchored VWAP - Which one is Better?

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: **VWAP** (volume weighted average price) — found under Indicators, resets every day / "only ranges for 24 hours" ([2:29]). **Anchored VWAP** — NOT an indicator, it is a drawing tool: "it's actually a tool in your toolbar the second icon down ... at the bottom you have anchored vwap" ([2:45]). Also **RSI** (used only for a divergence read; length/levels never stated), **parallel channels**, and the standard **volume bars** panel. No numeric settings are given for any of them.
- Setup/context required: Anchor the AVWAP at a **major high-volume pivot** — the biggest volume spike visible on the traditional volume panel, or the start of a major event: "you want big strong huge movements because that is going to be your pivot point" ([3:14]); "looking at the traditional volume charts you can clearly see right here the big spikes in volume that's where you want to keep your anchor points obviously you want to go with the biggest volume spike" ([5:00]). Then trade with the side of the AVWAP price is on: while price rejected off the AVWAP from below it was a downtrend; once price "crossed through it and are consistently pushing above it", flip to longs and drop a **second anchor at the low point** that started the new leg ([5:21]–[5:33]).
- Entry trigger: Two named entries: (1) **Mean-reversion to the VWAP** when price is "hyper extended away from the v-wap line" plus RSI divergence plus a trend-line break — "i would be very very comfortable putting in a long position here targeting the v-wap" ([4:32]). (2) **Break-and-retest of the AVWAP**: "it broke up retested it and is continuing to push up that right there is a one to two on a one hour time frame" ([4:46]). Otherwise, rejections off the AVWAP line in the direction of the trend ([3:28], [5:35]).
- Stop loss: "keeping your stop-loss below where the view app has been crossed recently" ([4:40]) — i.e. below the most recent AVWAP cross.
- Take profit: Target the VWAP/AVWAP line itself on the mean-reversion trade ([4:38]); the break-retest example is quoted as a **1:2** ([4:52]). No other target rule.
- Filters he adds: **Do not use standard VWAP on the daily chart** — "if you have this up on the daily chart it is not a good indication because the vwamp line basically resets each day so on a daily chart it's just pinning to each one of these candles" ([2:00]). Confluences he stacks on the long call: price making lower lows while RSI makes higher lows (regular bullish divergence, [4:08]–[4:16]), a broken 1-hour trend line in place since April 20th ([4:17]–[4:24]), "multiple divergences" ([4:27]), and hyper-extension from VWAP ([4:29]). Parallel-channel layering: "if it's ranging in one channel and then it breaks out of it it usually goes to the top of the second channel" ([1:16]).
