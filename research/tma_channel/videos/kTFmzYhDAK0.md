# How to Spot Reversals Using Volume and Support/Resistance Zones (Day Trading Strategy)
- id: kTFmzYhDAK0 | views: 26000 | length: 415s
- market(s) shown: EURUSD (daily and hourly); VIX referenced as the volatility index for indices
- timeframe(s) taught: Daily S/R for swing trading; **hourly** for the volume read; 15m/5m mentioned for intraday versions

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: **Volume** (standard, buy/sell coloured); **RSI** — length never stated, but he uses the **70/30 range** as the reference band ("it was within the 7030 range", [3:14]); **VIX / volatility index** — "more used for indices versus if you're looking at EuroUSD or any other currency pair" ([5:39]).
- Setup/context required: Mark S/R zones by finding **areas where price had a dramatic decrease or a dramatic increase from** — "all you have to do is find areas where the price had a dramatic decrease or a dramatic increase from, and that is your level" ([0:51]). Timeframe choice: **daily** S/R if swing trading, **hourly** pivots if scalping/intraday — explicitly *not* 5m pivots ([0:20]–[0:38]). Read the volume on the **hourly**, not the daily, because daily volume is near-constant and "doesn't really give you that much of an idea" ([1:47]).
- Entry trigger: The core rule — price arrives at the S/R zone on a **volume spike**, and then **volume dies off / flatlines while price does nothing**. That volume collapse = loss of interest = reversal. "when it hits that area, you want to see a spike up into the area and then the volume just die in comparison… see if the volume increased into the zone and then stopped when it got to that zone. That's when you want to get in on your buy or sell order" ([6:13]–[6:36]). Supporting candle condition: he looks for a **doji** at the zone (open and close exactly the same with a wick top and bottom, [1:16]–[1:23]).
- Stop loss: **none stated** anywhere in the video.
- Take profit: **none stated** anywhere in the video.
- Filters he adds:
  - **Double top / double bottom** at the zone (two-plus rejections of the same level) ([2:55], [6:26]).
  - **RSI divergence** as an added confluence on double tops: "two equal tops yet showing a divergence in the RSI. This is weakness in the price. Usually a sign of reversal" ([3:18]).
  - Confirmation of the break: once price **closes out of the zone**, volume should spike up on the breakout — "once it breaks below and the volume spikes up, you have interest in selling" ([2:42]).
  - Exhaustion read on the other side: repeated selling-volume spikes into the zone followed by **no interest to continue** = "the sellers are exhausted at that point… that's when the buyers have an opportunity to take over" ([5:21]–[5:31]).
  - VIX correlation (indices only): "as it loses its volume, the VIX will completely stop moving" — top = huge volume then very low volume ([5:43]–[6:07]).
