## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings:
  - **Fib retracement tool only** — "The fib tool is the third icon down, and you will see fib retracement" [0:31]. Settings are shown on screen, not spoken: "These are my settings. Take a screenshot, and it will look exactly like mine. I've also removed the background colors" [0:41]-[0:46].
  - **Fib levels actually used and spoken: 0.5, 0.618, and 1.0.**
- Setup/context required:
  - Trend + market structure: "If price is trending up, you want to mark every single high point... you are going to use the high points in an uptrend as your market structure" [0:10]-[0:17].
  - A trade only exists after a structure break: "anytime price breaks above that high, you're looking at Fibonacci levels" [0:23].
  - **Break must be a candle CLOSE:** "If a high is broken and a candle has closed above that high, then a retracement starts" [0:55]-[1:02].
  - You do not draw the fib until a retracement has actually begun: "until we start getting a retracement, we don't draw our fib tool" [1:22]-[1:27].
- Entry trigger: **Resting limit order placed between the 0.5 and the 0.618** — "set a limit order in between the 0.5 and the 618" [1:08]-[1:15]; restated as "limit order halfway between" [1:50]. No candle confirmation is required; it is a passive limit fill.
- Stop loss: **Halfway between the 0.618 and the 1.0** — "Stop loss halfway between the 618 and the one" [1:33]; restated "stop-loss set in between the 618 and the one" [1:50]. (i.e. ≈ the 0.809 level.)
- Take profit: **The previous high** (for longs) — "your take-profit is the previous high" [1:36], restated [1:50]. Shorts mirror it ("You do the exact same thing for short positions" [1:58]) → previous low.
- Filters he adds:
  - **Fib anchoring includes wicks:** "Drop up your fib zone from the bottom to the top of the move. That means including wick from the bottom to the top" [1:02]-[1:08]. For shorts: "Draw the fib tool from the top to the bottom" [1:27].
  - No-fill is acceptable and not chased: "If your limit order doesn't get hit, that's fine. Move on to the next one" [2:04].
  - Frequency caveat: "It's not going to happen all the time, but when it does, it's amazing" [0:27].
