- market(s) shown: Bitcoin [0:46, 0:58]
- timeframe(s) taught: 15 minute for the setup ("looking at the price of bitcoin recently on the 15 minute time frame, zooming out" [1:01-1:04]), but held as a multi-day SWING trade — "you are not scalping this, you are holding this trade for days and days and days" [2:48-2:54]

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: RSI (length NOT stated). He quotes readings: "at this point right here on the RSI we're at 26, below the traditional like 30 level" [2:16-2:22]; and on the pullbacks "the RSI was at the 70 level here and 68 here" [4:08-4:12]. Plus hand-drawn order blocks [1:04-1:09].
- Setup/context required: a **rounded top** — "this is what's known as a rounded top ... nice and arched" [1:16-1:22] — forming while the RSI declines steadily underneath it: "the price seems to be consolidating at this level making a tiny bit of an arch, but if you look at the RSI it's literally just going down and down and down" [1:33-1:43]. Explicit anti-rule: do NOT read the low RSI as oversold-buy — "don't use the RSI as overbought and oversold because ... a market can stay oversold or overbought for a very long period of time and buying when it's that low can really screw you" [1:55-2:11].
- Entry trigger: not stated as a candle/event. The trade is set up on the confluence of (rounded top + falling RSI + next order block below); "theoretically this is what the trade would look like when you're setting it up" [2:42-2:44]. Direction: short.
- Stop loss: quoted as an absolute distance on BTC — "the stop loss on this is 1,571 points on bitcoin" [3:04-3:11].
- Take profit: "your profit target is 5,600 points, targeting this next order block" [3:11-3:17]. → implied ~3.56R. Target rule in words: the next order block below.
- Filters he adds: the order block below is treated as a barrier — "it is probably not going to push through this order block with what you're seeing going on" [2:35-2:40]. Trade management: "set your trade and forget about it" [3:34-3:36].
