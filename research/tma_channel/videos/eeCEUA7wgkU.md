## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: none required. Volume is used as a breakout confirmation, but no volume indicator, lookback or threshold is specified [5:40-6:09].
- Setup/context required: identified support/resistance ZONES (not lines) — "Support and resistance are zones, not lines" [2:34-2:38].
- Entry trigger: a three-step method, stated verbatim:
  1. "look at where the price has bounced or dropped from before. These are zones of interest. Mark them with a box, not a line" [3:06-3:18]
  2. "wait for the price to come back to that zone" [3:19-3:22]
  3. "watch how it reacts" [3:22-3:27]
  Reaction taxonomy given: "If price stalls, wicks, forms a big candle, buyers and sellers might be stepping in. If it breaks through with power, that level might flip sides." [3:27-3:41]
  Governing rule: "Don't trade the level. Trade the reaction at the level." [2:52-2:58]
- Stop loss: not stated.
- Take profit: not stated.
- Filters he adds: volume filter on breakouts — "A strong breakout means strong volume, and weak volume means be careful" [6:01-6:09]; "If price breaks through a level and volume spikes up, that means a lot of people are behind that move ... if price breaks a level and volume is low or falling, that's a red flag" [5:42-5:59]. Also an anti-filter: "if you try to trade every support bounce or every resistance touch, you will lose" [2:44-2:52].
