## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
Two strategies are summarised; each is taught in full in a separate linked video.

### Strategy 1 — Overnight Range Breakout
- Indicators + exact settings: none — just a box drawn on the chart.
- Setup/context required: **draw a box around the overnight session — from the END of the New York session to the OPEN of the London session — spanning that period's HIGH to LOW** [0:29]-[0:52]. No clock times or timezone are given, only the session boundaries.
- Entry trigger: **break out above the box high -> go long; break out below the box low -> go short** [0:52]-[0:59]. Both directions are traded, i.e. a stop-and-reverse style setup where a failed first breakout is expected.
- Stop loss: not stated in this video (deferred to the dedicated video).
- Take profit: **1 : 1.5 risk-to-reward** [1:11]-[1:19].
- Filters he adds: none stated. Stated rationale: "it doesn't work out every single day, but statistically over time, when price breaks out of this, it usually continues in that direction for the majority of the day" [0:59]-[1:11]; and because both directions are traded at 1:1.5, "even if you lose one and win the other, you're still up half a percent for the day".

### Strategy 2 — 30-minute Happy Trail "Case 4"
- Indicators + exact settings: **Happy Trail indicator, "Case 4" preset, all other settings left DEFAULT**, on the **30-minute** timeframe [1:26]-[1:38]. Happy Trail is his proprietary paid indicator; "Case 4" is not explained.
- Setup/context required: none — every print is taken.
- Entry trigger: **whenever it prints below price, take a long; whenever it prints above price, take a short. Enter at the CLOSE of the candle** [1:38]-[2:00].
- Stop loss: **a few ticks above the top of the signal candle for a short** (a couple of ticks below the low of the candle for a long) [2:00]-[2:07], [3:41]-[3:47].
- Take profit: **1 : 1.5 risk-to-reward** [2:19]-[2:26]. (He says "1:5" at [2:07] — a transcript/speech slip; both the preceding and following statements say 1 to 1.5.)
- Filters he adds: none.

### Risk / trade-management rules stated (apply to both)
- **New account protocol: place the first trade at 0.01 lots as a test to verify the contract size matches expectations before sizing up** [2:26]-[2:43].
- **Scaling in**: enter a small test position (1 lot), then add the full position (5 lots) on the pullback [3:04]-[3:27].
- **Break-even rule: "once I get to a one to one risk-to-reward, I like to move my stops to break even"** to preserve capital against an aggressive pullback [3:41]-[4:00]. In the example this scratched a trade that was ~$300 in profit when a big candle swept back through break-even.
