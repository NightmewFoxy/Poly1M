## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
A fully rules-based signal-following system built on a proprietary indicator. Zero technical analysis by design.

- Indicators + exact settings: **"Happy Trail"** indicator from **trdfloor.com** [0:27, 5:02]. "There are four case profiles with this indicator. I am using the default profile of **case 4**. All the settings are set to default." [0:33]. He enables **signal repetition** deliberately [0:34] so his phone alerts while the candle is still forming, giving him time to watch the close.
- Setup/context required: **none** — [0:57] "this is a strategy with no technical analysis. You don't have to do anything except stay at the signal." No trend filter, no structure, no session.
- Entry trigger:
  - **Short:** an **upside-down smiley face prints above the candle** → enter short **at the close of that candle** (and only if the signal is still printed when the candle closes) [1:02, 0:45].
  - **Long:** a **smiley face prints below the candle** → enter long at the close of the candle [1:17].
- Stop loss: **one tick above the top of the signal candle** for shorts; **one tick below the bottom of the signal candle** for longs [1:12, 1:29]. Never moved [1:49] "You don't move your stop loss."
- Take profit: **1 : 2 risk-to-reward, fixed, on every trade** [1:15, 1:38, 2:14].
- Filters he adds: **none.** Explicitly take every signal regardless of recent losses [2:45] "It doesn't matter if you've had four consecutive losses. you continue to do the strategy over and over."

Claimed results (his own, since Oct 1, ~1.5 months, GER40 30m): **12 winners / 11 losers**; 12 wins × 2R = +24%, 11 losses × 1R = −11%; **net +13%** [4:30]. Framed as beating the S&P 500's 9–10%/yr [4:49]. Says the same test should be re-run on whatever asset the viewer trades [4:57].

Behavioural rules stated: no greed / no scaling out of big moves [2:08]; no adjustments mid-trade [2:23]; expect stop-outs one tick from the turn and keep going [2:51].
