# Where to Place your Stop Loss and Take Profit Tutorial

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: three moving averages — **21, 50, 200 period** ([0:29] "we have the 21 period the 50 period and the 200 period"). Type is **not stated in this video** (the channel's other videos use smoothed/SMMA; he points to his scalping video for the setup).
- Setup/context required: A **clearly trending market with fanned-out moving averages** — price trading below the 21 for a downtrend (only shorts) or above it for an uptrend. [2:01] "you want clear and concise movement of fanned out moving averages you want them all spread apart because that's showing momentum you only want to trade momentum".
- Entry trigger: A **rejection off the 21 moving average** in the direction of the trend — [0:41] "look for respecting the 21 moving average and rejecting off of it down that's where you want to place your trades". In a downtrend, enter short at the 21 on the rejection; only shorts while below the 21.
- Stop loss: **comfortably above the next moving average out** — i.e. entering at the 21, put the stop **above the 50** ([1:29] "you keep your stop loss above the 50 level"). Generalised at [2:21]: "keep your stop loss above the next moving average". He notes you don't strictly *have* to go all the way to the 50, but that going to the 50 is how you "stay comfortable and never get stopped out" ([0:57]).
- Take profit: **stop-loss distance × 2** — [2:23] "whatever that number is multiply it by two and that's your take profit level". Stated repeatedly as a **1:2 risk-to-reward ratio, minimum** ([1:32], [1:48], [2:55], [3:06]).
- Filters he adds:
  - **No-trade filter:** when moving averages start **intertwining** (consolidation), walk away — [1:53] "when the price starts consolidating and you see this intertwining of moving averages walk away from this pair do not trade this do not trade this ever".
  - **Ignore prior market structure** when a market is trending hard: [2:14] "do not look at previous market structure of like higher highs what when you're seeing a massively trending market like this". (Note: this contradicts his other videos, which make market structure a required confluence — e.g. `K7vFNn7fZ7Y`, `R3T1zRyZdMc`.)
  - **Set and forget:** [2:34] "set your stop loss set your take profit set your trade walk away don't even look at it". If stopped out, take it and look for the next trade.
  - **Bias flip rule:** if price starts trading above the previous moving average, start looking for longs instead ([3:00]).
