## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings:
  - **21, 50 and 200 SMOOTHED moving averages** ([4:33] "I'm going to turn on the 21, the 50 and 200 smooth moving averages").
  - **RSI with a 50 level only** — the channel's custom single-line RSI with no overbought/oversold bands. Length unstated. [7:02] "It does not show overbought and oversold areas because I don't use the RSI like that and neither should you."
  - Channel's free all-in-one indicator, which also flags **engulfing candles** and **three-line strikes** (double-arrow markers) and shades the **London / Tokyo / New York** session.
- Setup/context required (his stated "rules that must be met"):
  1. **Trade with the trend only** — rule number one. Longs only when price is **above the 200 SMMA** and the MAs stack in order (21 above 50 above 200, all rising); shorts only when price is **below the 200 SMMA** with the stack inverted (200, 50, 21 descending). [4:41]–[5:07].
  2. **Trade only inside your session window** (his is London) — [4:16] "this is my trading window. I'm not looking at any of this... I'm actively trading during this time."
  3. 1h S/R marked first: horizontal lines at major highs/lows, **two above and two below** current price, plus minor consolidation levels in a different colour ([2:02]–[2:52]).
- Entry trigger: **Confluence 1** — an **engulfing candle or a three-line strike** in the trend direction (red signals ignored in an uptrend and vice versa). **Confluence 2** — the **RSI line must be above the 50 level and rising** for longs (below 50 and falling for shorts). [6:23]–[6:37]. Enter **at the close of the signal candle / open of the next**: [7:49] "when this candle closes right here, that's when this Arrow becomes permanent. And that's when you want to enter your trade." He states these two confluences alone are enough with the trend, but he would want at least four confluences for a counter-trend trade ([7:09]–[7:23]). Optional third confluence: a recent hidden bullish divergence on the RSI ([7:23]–[7:47]).
- Stop loss: **2× the length of the entry (engulfing) candle**, which he says coincides with the swing low. [8:02] "you can actually keep your stop-loss twice the length of the [candle] which consequently is the swing low." Worked example: **8 pips stop**.
- Take profit: **2:1 risk-to-reward, minimum, always pre-set.** [8:12] "what I want you guys to do, when entering your trade is a [2]:1 risk-to-reward ratio. This must be set every single time." Worked example: **16 pips TP against the 8-pip stop.** Two alternative managements offered: (a) **close 80% of the position at the 2R** and let the remainder run to the next major S/R level (his example named the level **0.74234**); (b) hold until a **regular bearish divergence** prints (price higher highs, RSI lower highs) ([10:28]–[11:02]).
- Filters he adds:
  - Session gate (London in his case; the indicator also offers Tokyo/New York).
  - Nearby S/R above the entry in the trade direction is an extra confluence that the target will be reached ([8:41]–[9:05]).
  - **Risk 1% of account per trade**, targeting 2% per trade; example: $1,000 account → $10 risk, $20 target. He models 1 trade/day × 3 days/week = 6%/week ≈ 24%/month ([9:22]–[10:03]).
  - **No-trade days**: if price is below the 200 SMMA and the MAs are not lined up, do not trade that day ([11:18]–[11:44]).
  - Never move the stop loss; never risk money you can't lose ([13:07]–[13:36]).
