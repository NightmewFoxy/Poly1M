# The Surprising Truth About Finding the Right Market Trend

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: RSI — "the relative strength index", explicitly "the standard RSI indicator", **length/levels never stated** [6:41]-[8:36]. He briefly loads a multi-oscillator tool ("any oscillator underlay" by "the trading floor", set to show RSI + MACD) then abandons it for the plain RSI at [8:16] "sorry I'm just going to use the regular RSI because it will be way easier". Also mentions a "three line strike" indicator (searched as "three LS", "my ttf") at [15:25]-[15:36] — settings unstated. Cosmetic setting given exactly: change candle body/border/wick colors from red/green to **yellow and white** via right-click > Settings > Appearance > Symbol [0:28]-[1:13].
- Setup/context required (stated sequence):
  1. On the **1h or 4h**, mark horizontal rays at major pivot points; prefer **big round numbers** ("banks want to trade at big round numbers", "psychological levels / bank levels") — worked examples spoken: 0.7000 [2:03], "75.50 / 75550" [2:55], "just below 7300" [3:28].
  2. Box any area of heavy consolidation and treat it as a **no-trade zone** ("better to stay away from it") [3:48]-[4:13].
  3. Draw trend lines at approximately a **45 degree angle**; to normalise the scale, "get all the candles in the area and then double click on where the price is on the chart on the right hand side" to reset the standard aspect ratio [4:20]-[6:11].
  4. Determine trend by market structure: higher highs + higher lows = uptrend; the trend is only considered broken when price **stops making higher highs and starts making lower highs** (break of structure) [10:00]-[10:22]. "Until that happens you must assume that this price will maintain its upwards trajectory."
  5. Check for **regular divergence** on RSI at the swing high/low: "price made a higher high yet RSI made a lower high" [9:12]-[9:21]. If no divergence on the 1h, step out to the 4h to check there [8:51]-[9:00].
  6. Only after direction is fixed on the higher timeframe do you drop to 15m/5m [10:57]-[11:16]. He recommends 15m over 5m: "you can see a lot more noise [on 5m], which is why I still suggest you use the 15 minute" [11:48]-[11:55]; also "the candle formations themselves mean more and carry more weight at 15 minutes than they do on the five minute chart" [11:25].
- Entry trigger: on the lower timeframe, in the direction of the higher-timeframe trend, at a support/resistance rejection or a break of structure, look for **one of**: RSI oversold/overbought, hidden bullish divergence, a **three-line strike**, or a doji / engulfing candle [16:22]-[18:38] and [15:00]-[15:17]. Worked long example at [15:38]-[15:50]: after price broke through a level (resistance flipped to support), "you want to look for the three line strike at this zone... so you got one right here — if we took a long position there".
- Stop loss: two distinct rules stated.
  - Default scalp: **10 pip stop loss** [16:01] — but he qualifies this applies "when you're getting an actual pivot point... from a higher high and a higher low, not when you're rejecting a support and resistance level" [16:06]-[16:14].
  - At an S/R rejection: place the stop **below the prior low that was already wick-swept** — "we had a low right here and then it dipped below that so you know that this was the liquidity grab off of this wick... put it below this one" [16:16]-[16:36].
- Take profit: **20 pip take profit** paired with the 10 pip stop (i.e. 1:2) [16:01]. Restated as a ratio rule: "you want a 1 to 1.5 or a one to two risk to reward, anything more than that is not scalping" [16:36]-[16:44]. Price targets on the higher timeframe = the horizontal round-number lines drawn in step 1 [10:23]-[10:37]. He explicitly rejects trailing stops: "I've been screwed over so many times holding a trade for two days that I just... I don't like it" [17:01]-[17:11].
- Filters he adds:
  - No trading inside marked consolidation boxes; no trading while price is "dancing on" a trend line — "we don't want to trade in these zones, we want to wait for price to break this downtrend" [9:22]-[9:35].
  - Avoid choppy price action; only trade "nice even higher highs and higher lows... really smooth, really consistent" [14:01]-[14:28].
  - News: notes NFP caused a large move and that AUDUSD is therefore "not in the trading area for me" [10:39]-[10:55].
  - Routine: analyse on Sunday night / Monday with all drawings deleted for a bias-free read [13:17]-[13:36].
  - Never start on the low timeframe — doing so inverts your bias against the higher-timeframe trend [19:01]-[19:33].
