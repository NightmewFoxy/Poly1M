- Setup/context required:
  - **Session opening-hour range = the first four candles of the session's opening hour**: "this is Tokyo session's opening hour, these first four candles basically create a range that if it breaks out of, it will usually continue in that direction of the breakout for the remainder of the day" [4:14]-[4:28]. He applies it to **London and New York, not Tokyo**: "not looking at the Tokyo session, but just checking out the range right here of the London session" [4:36]-[4:44]. (On 15m, four candles = one hour — consistent.)
  - **Trend/channel:** draw a parallel channel on the trend; clone it to build stacked ranges — "click your parallel channel, hit the three dots and hit clone... you can overlay it just above it and that's your new range" [7:09]-[7:23].
  - **Trend confirmation via the 200:** "once price broke the 200 and previous price structure to the left, I knew that we were going to be in a good downtrend" [5:07]-[5:16].
  - **PD array:** Gann box drawn on the structure-breaking move; in a downtrend "price wants to pull back up into an area of premium; you want to sell things at a premium price and you want to buy things at a discount price, so I'm only looking for short positions" [5:41]-[5:55].
  - **Channel-position bias (stated as the general rule):** "if we're at the bottom of the range we're looking for long positions; if we're at the top of the range we're looking for sell positions" [9:05]-[9:12].
- Entry trigger: **the Happy Trail signal, taken only when it fires as price breaks out of the session opening-hour range in the direction of the PD-array/channel bias** — "for a sell position it gives you an upside down smiley face, and that happened right as it broke out of the range: you enter in a short position" [8:45]-[8:52]. Stated as a formula: "if you look at the New York open or the London open — that range — and then you find something breaking out in the direction of your bias, and your trade entry is the happy trail signal, the likelihood of your success is substantially higher because you have multiple confluences" [9:13]-[9:28].
- Stop loss: **above the previous swing** — "with a stop loss above the previous swing" [8:52]-[8:54]. Then aggressively moved: "after a trade got into profit I immediately started moving my stop loss up; if I could get it to break even as quickly as possible I was happy" [2:33]-[2:41]; "immediately move my trade to break even" [3:10]-[3:12].
