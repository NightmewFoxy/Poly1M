# Price Action Trading Strategy (loss of momentum)
- id: qWgOLhR8r9U | views: 108000 | length: 341s
- market(s) shown: AUDUSD (Australian dollar / US dollar) [0:48]; claimed to work on "crypto currencies... indices or forex" [0:36]-[0:41]
- timeframe(s) taught: **15 minute** — "so on a 15 minute time frame we are seeing a movement down to that zone" [2:42]-[2:49]. Holding period observed: "the overall move actually took one day and 22 hours" [4:45]-[4:51].

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: **none — pure price action.** Only hand-drawn support/resistance zones ("draw out your bracket and actually put a level here of known support" [0:53]-[1:01]).
- Setup/context required:
  1. A support (or resistance) **zone** confirmed by **at least three prior rejections** — "price rejected here, price rejected here and price had a very big rejection here" [1:14]-[1:19]. That count is the closest thing to a numeric zone-validity rule in the video.
  2. Price must be **approaching that zone with decaying momentum**: large candles first, then progressively smaller ones. "big ass candles is large momentum but as the candles get smaller as it gets closer to a level of support and resistance you know that you're getting a loss of momentum" [2:01]-[2:17].
  3. He names this as the whole point of the video: "this right here is all i want you to learn out of this video — you want to see a loss of momentum coming up to a support and resistance zone" [2:57]-[3:06].
- Entry trigger: **A big rejection candle off the zone; enter on the CLOSE of that candle.** "once we get to that zone and have a big rejection candle off of it that is when you want to enter into your position and wait for the close of the candle" [3:08]-[3:19]. Hard gate stated at [2:32]-[2:42]: "you do not get into a trade unless you see a firm rejection off of that zone with the loss of momentum leading up to it."
- Stop loss: two variants, both stated:
  - Default: **below the swing low** — "your stop loss is going to be below the swing low, especially knowing that this was a stop loss hunt by the market makers" [1:42]-[1:49].
  - When the rejection candle is unusually large: **just below the zone** is enough — "because the size of this candle is so massive your stop loss does not need to be below that previous stop hunt, just below the zone is completely fine" [3:19]-[3:30].
- Take profit: **scaled, two targets, both placed at the price where price has traded/crossed the MOST — not at extremes.**
  - TP1: "your take profit should be at a level where price has hit the most so we're looking at **36 pips** right here, a **1 to 1.5 risk to reward** ratio" [3:30]-[3:39].
  - TP2: further left, again at a high-traffic price rather than the swing high — "having these high points as your target is a little bit more risky than getting in wherever the price crossed the most and had the most resistance" [4:01]-[4:12]; "your second take profit level can be right there... which leaves you a total take profit level of **80 pips**, which is a **one to three point risk to reward ratio**" [4:14]-[4:28].
- Filters he adds:
  - **Pre-trade R/R screen: reject the trade unless you can get at least 1:2 R/R, and unless TP is worth ≥20-50 pips.** "I want you to look at the parameters to see if you could at least get a **one to two risk to reward ratio** out of this trade, and if your take profit level is even worth taking **20 to 50 pips or more**" [1:29]-[1:42]. (Note the internal contradiction: the example he then trades is only 1:1.5 to TP1.)
  - Accepting 1:1.5 on TP1 is justified by the market being in a **consolidation range**: "which is completely acceptable in this scenario because you do not know if this is going to break out of this consolidation range that it is technically in" [3:41]-[3:50].
  - **Mentality filter:** "when trading like this you shouldn't have a scalping mentality, you should have more of a swing trading mentality catching a long trend up" [4:28]-[4:36].
  - Long lone spikes are dismissed as **news events or stop hunts** and treated as noise when siting the level: "anytime you see anything like this, this was either a news event or a stop hunt" [1:03]-[1:12].
  - Extra confluence borrowed from an earlier video: "that loss of momentum leading into **multiple wicks on the downside** is a nice signal" [5:07]-[5:16].
