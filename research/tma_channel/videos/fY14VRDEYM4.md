# Day Trading the High Impact New Events

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: none. Two **tools**, not indicators:
  1. TradingView **economic events on chart**: right-click chart → Settings → **Events tab → "show economic events on the chart"**; coloured flags then appear at the bottom of the chart marking news releases [1:01–1:26].
  2. **MyFXBook economic calendar** (google "my fx book economic calendar") — lists every event, its release time, and its **low / medium / high impact** rating, plus **previous** and **consensus/forecast** numbers [1:32–2:12, 7:47–8:04].
- Setup/context required: n/a — this is an avoidance rule, not an entry system.
- Entry trigger: none. The rule is the **opposite**: **do not enter**.
- Stop loss: **always use one** — the whole risk argument of the video. He demonstrates two deliberately stop-less trades to show how far an account can go negative in 60 seconds [4:49–5:14].
- Take profit: n/a.
- Filters he adds (the actual content):
  - **News filter, hard version**: before every entry, check the MyFXBook calendar for the currencies in your pair; if a **high-impact** event is imminent, don't take the trade [2:27–2:53]. His personal rule: "**if I see a little red high thing I shut my laptop and I go on a bike ride I'm not trading on high impact news events ever**" [8:56–9:06].
  - **Non-farm payroll** = the **first Friday of every month**; "this will make anything that you're trading spike **100 pips in either direction**" [7:31–7:46].
  - Only trade news if you have years of macro/fundamental education (GDP, unemployment, central-bank printing, inflation, cross-country correlations); otherwise "literally don't even bother" [2:53–3:40].
  - **Currency-correlation caveat**: a news spike does not move everything the same way — GBPUSD ripped up while USD/PLN was in a strong downtrend [4:02–4:24].
  - **Expected vs unexpected news**: calendar events are expected (you know the time); geopolitical shocks (Russia invading Ukraine) are not, and produce candles you cannot react to.
  - Direction is **not** predictable from the number alone: "if the actual number ... is higher or lower than this number it will dramatically impact which way the price goes and sometimes it's counterintuitive" [8:00–8:10].
