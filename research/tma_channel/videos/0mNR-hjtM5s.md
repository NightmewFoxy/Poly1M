## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
This video is Arty teaching ICT's (Inner Circle Trader) Silver Bullet, not his own strategy. It is unusually mechanical for this channel.
- Indicators + exact settings: NONE. Pure price action + a session/time filter. A vertical line marks midnight.
- Setup/context required:
  1. **Time window is the whole filter.** All times are NEW YORK time — he states it explicitly: "everything that this guy teaches on his YouTube channel is always New York Times so that is UTC minus four or whatever it is during daylight savings time" [0:53]-[1:01].
  2. **London Silver Bullet window: 3:00am - 4:00am New York time.** "this is where you want the setup to happen; if it doesn't happen in this time you don't trade it" [1:09]-[1:17]. (He says the setup "only happens one hour per session" [0:47]-[0:50]; only the London window's exact hours are given in this video.)
  3. Within that hour, an aggressive impulsive move (down, for the short example).
  4. That move must contain a **fair value gap (FVG)**, defined explicitly: within THREE candles, if the wick of candle 1 and the wick of candle 3 have a space between them, that space is the FVG [1:17]-[1:31].
- Entry trigger: price retraces back INTO the fair value gap zone during the 3am-4am hour -> enter short (mirror for longs) [1:31]-[1:39].
- Stop loss: above the swing high of the impulsive move — "just to be safe you put your stop loss above that swing high" [1:39]-[1:43].
- Take profit:
  - First TP = the swing low of the impulsive move [1:43]-[1:46].
  - Further targets = liquidity below wicks to the left (prior wick lows) [1:56]-[2:02].
  - R multiples he quotes for those extended targets: **1:2.7 or 1:1.56** [2:02]-[2:07].
- Filters he adds — minimum distance filter (the most valuable number in the video):
  - **FX ("4X") pair: if the distance from the fair value gap to the swing low is less than 15 pips, don't take the trade** [1:46]-[1:53].
  - **Index: if it's less than 10 (points), don't take it** [1:53]-[1:55].
  - **No-setup = no-trade day: "this setup does not happen every day; if it doesn't happen you don't trade that day"** [2:05]-[2:09].
