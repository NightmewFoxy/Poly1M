## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings (**three indicators**):
  1. **TMA Overlay** (publicly searchable on TradingView; in the description of every video) [1:17]-[1:29]. He changes its settings: **"get rid of the 100 smoothed moving averages, so you're only going to have the 21, the 50 and the 200 smoothed moving averages"** [2:12]-[2:20]. He then states exactly what the indicator is: "that's all this indicator is — a **200, a 50 and a 21** [smoothed MAs], as well as **bullish and bearish engulfing candles** and **double arrows for these three line strikes**" [2:20]-[2:33].
     - **Three-line strike definition given verbatim:** "which means those was **three consecutive in one direction and one engulfing in the opposite direction**" [2:33]-[2:39].
     - MA type is explicitly **SMMA (smoothed)**, lengths **21 / 50 / 200**; the 100 is removed.
  2. **TMA RSI Divergence indicator** — taken down by TradingView, script given in the description [0:53]-[1:02], [1:30]-[1:44]. "this basically gives you an RSI line with **hidden bullish, hidden bearish, regular bullish and regular bearish** divergences. **They do print late** so you need to be able to identify them live and wait for **trend line breaks**" [1:44]-[1:57]. RSI length is never stated.
  3. **VWAP** — TradingView built-in, "go to the indicators tab and just type in vwap" [4:04]-[4:21]. Settings he changes are cosmetic only: "get rid of the **upper band and the lower band and the fill color**, and change the color... **second thickness**" [4:21]-[4:35]. No anchor/period change.
- Setup/context required (the sequence he gives at [5:09]-[5:46] as "from beginning to end"):
  1. **Price must CROSS the 200 SMMA** — "we are waiting for price to break over the 200 moving averages or under the 200... **it needs to cross**. So once it crosses you're looking for long positions on the upside, and once it falls below you're looking for shorts on the downside" [5:11]-[5:31].
  2. **The 21 and the 50 must also cross through** — "you want the 21 and the 50 moving average to also cross through, you want nice momentum to the upside when we're changing directions in the trend" [5:31]-[5:41]; the stack must be "all lined up" [5:41]-[5:46].
  3. **RSI above the 50 level** for longs (from the prior version, restated at [2:54]-[2:59] and [6:08]-[6:12]: "you just want to keep above the 50 line here").
  4. Optional/extra: a **hidden bullish divergence** already showing [6:00]-[6:08].
- Entry trigger: **A rejection off the 21 SMMA that prints as an engulfing candle or a three-line strike** — "you want to look for **rejections off of the 21** with these engulfing candles or three line strikes... at any of these points you can get into a trade **after a three line strike or an engulfing candle**" [2:48]-[3:04].
  - **NEW in this video — the VWAP confluence:** the rejection should happen off the 21 **AND** the VWAP at the same time. "if you can get that in confluence with the vwap line, the vwap holding the price like being a nice level of support... and you get the vwap rejection right there, that is an extra confluence" [4:41]-[5:09]. Cost of the extra confluence is stated: "you had to wait an extra **one or two candles**" [6:12]-[6:16].
- Stop loss: **at the previous moving average** — "set your stop loss at the **previous moving average**" [3:04]-[3:06]. In his own counter-trend example: "stoploss around where the mush of moving averages is" [9:28]-[9:33].
- Take profit: **look left to the next resistance/support zone** — "for your take profit you are going to look left and see where the **next resistance zone** was and that's going to be your scalp" [3:06]-[3:16]; restated "you're always going to look left to see where it had the most resistance" [8:37]-[8:42].
  - Example R multiples given: **1:3** on a 21-rejection long, a **127 point move on US30** [3:16]-[3:32]; and **1:2.25** on his counter-trend short [9:42]-[9:49].
- Filters he adds:
  - **Don't take the trade too early in the cross.** "you can see right here it rejected off of the vwap line as it crossed through the 200 before it rejected off of the 21 — **I don't really suggest getting into these trades that are that early**, just because I want to see the other moving averages get above it to show that nice momentum" [6:59]-[7:19].
  - **Multi-timeframe 200 check:** "I'm always looking at multiple time frames making sure that I'm above or below the 200 on those time frames" [7:46]-[7:56].
  - **Ignore some pattern prints:** "in my 5 minute video I explained how a lot of these bullish and bearish engulfing candles as well as three line strikes **should be ignored** while looking at price action" [2:01]-[2:12] — the criterion is deferred to that video.
  - Divergences **print late**; identify them live and **wait for trend line breaks** [1:50]-[1:57].
  - **Counter-trend variant** (he flags it as not for everyone): scalp *between* the moving averages — "you wait for the price to cross through those moving averages to push down to the 200, but with this you also get that **vwap cross** showing you that momentum to the downside, so **as it crosses the 50** you can easily take that position **down to the 200**" [8:42]-[9:02]. His own version targeted a zone **below** the 200 and he calls that "a little bit more risky" [9:05]-[9:14].
  - Short-side mirror stated explicitly: "it fell below the 200 moving average, rejected off of the 21, then found that push down as it passed the vwap line, and right there you're going to get in on your short position" [8:25]-[8:37].
  - His own trade before recording: entry on "this huge ass candle... breaking down below that vwap" [9:16]-[9:28] — i.e. a large candle closing through VWAP is treated as an entry in the counter-trend variant.
