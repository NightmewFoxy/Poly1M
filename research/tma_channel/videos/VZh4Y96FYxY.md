## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: volume bars only (no length/type/threshold given). No MA, no oscillator.
- Setup/context required: Identify a consolidation range where price is flat but volume is high. [3:16] "how do you know if price is consolidating it's because it's flat and volume is massively high that's how you know." A range at the bottom of a downtrend = accumulation (look long); a range at the top of an uptrend = distribution (look short).
- Entry trigger: Wait for the "spring" — the false breakout out of the range on a volume spike — and then the genuine break out of the range. Also stated: [5:02] "this is why you want to enter your trade at the top of this range and not worry about these breakouts". Mirror for distribution: after the range plus a high-volume spring, go short as the markdown starts ([5:43]–[5:48]).
