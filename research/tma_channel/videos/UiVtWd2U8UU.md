## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
A confluence-stacking framework rather than a single entry system. He opens by conceding fib alone is weak: [0:00] "Fibonacci retracements ... are a little bit weak, and you can't always rely on them."

- Indicators + exact settings:
  - **Fibonacci retracement tool.** Levels explicitly named: **0.618** (the "golden"/"gold zone" level, used repeatedly) and **0.5** [2:34]. No other levels used; the fib is drawn swing-low to swing-high (anchoring never spelled out).
  - **RSI** — settings never stated in this video.
- Setup/context required: an existing trend with clean structure. For the bullish example: **higher highs and higher lows, and you only look at the higher lows** [1:40].
- Entry trigger — three stackable confluences, all landing on the same level:
  1. **Fib 0.618 + hidden RSI divergence.** Hidden bullish divergence is defined [1:03]: "price makes a higher low, but the RSI makes a lower low. It signals a monstrous trend continuation." Rule: [1:18] "if your pullback lands right in that 0.618 zone and the RSI shows a hidden divergence, that's a golden entry signal." **Hidden divergence, not regular divergence, is what he says matters here.**
  2. **Fib level + a tested support/resistance zone.** [2:22] "when a fib retracement lands exactly in a strong zone that's been tested before, that's a confluence" — e.g. the 0.5 lining up with an S/R zone built from the last two swing lows. Demonstrated case [3:00]: a multi-touch support broke with momentum, price retraced, and rejected at the 0.618 which was also the old S/R zone.
  3. **Fib level + the previous day's high or low.** Mark each day separately [4:17]. [3:43] "you draw a fib retracement and the 0.618 line lines up exactly with yesterday's low. That's ... two layers of liquidity stacking." Demonstrated sequence [4:29]: previous day's low → momentum swing up → hard pullback that **sweeps liquidity below the previous day's low** and rejects off the fib gold zone → bullish for the next day and a half (24,000 → 24,490 on the hourly).
  4. **All three at once** = the A+ setup [5:02].
