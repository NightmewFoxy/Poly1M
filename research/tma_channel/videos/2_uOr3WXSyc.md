## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: **RSI** (for the divergence exit rule). Length and levels NEVER stated. No moving averages used in this video. Chart setup tip: change candle colours to something neutral — "red and yellow are trigger colors for the human psyche" [0:20]-[0:33].
- Setup/context required (the top-down sequence):
  1. **Daily**: draw horizontal lines at major price points where price reversed; draw trend lines on the most current trend plus one horizontal ray. Establish bias. In the example: a major support was broken and is now used as resistance -> "overall my bias is down" [0:18]-[1:23]. Define the trading window (entry zone -> profit target) at this level.
  2. **1 hour**: confirm. Price had made higher highs/higher lows, broke the higher-timeframe downtrend, and now trades below both -> "more confirmation of our bias going down" [1:33]-[1:47].
  3. **Change of character requirement**: "once price starts making higher highs and higher lows and then starts reversing, you want to see a break of previous structure. So currently this is a double bottom, this has not changed character yet" [1:47]-[2:01] — i.e. a double bottom alone is NOT a reversal; you need the structure break.
  4. **15 minute**: place a horizontal ray at the rejection low; that broken support becomes potential resistance = **the area of interest**.
  5. **5 minute**: wait for price to reach the area of interest and print a rejection candle pattern.
- Entry trigger: a candlestick pattern INSIDE the marked area of interest — he names: **three-line strike, doji, engulfing, morning star** [2:19]-[2:28], [3:41]-[3:47]. The worked example uses the three-line strike, defined explicitly: **three bullish candles followed by one bearish engulfing candle** (for a short) [4:24]-[4:41].
- Stop loss: **above the swing high of the three-line strike** [4:50]-[4:54].
- Take profit: **1:2 risk-to-reward** [4:54]-[4:57], targeting the prior low ("take profit one assuming another bottom") [2:32]-[2:35]. Claim: "a three line strike is going to get you your 1 to 2 a majority of the time" [5:23]-[5:27].
- **Exit / trade-extension rule (the most mechanical rule in the video)** [5:27]-[5:51]:
  - To stay in the trade beyond 1:2, hold while price makes lower lows AND RSI makes lower lows (in a short).
  - **The first time price makes a lower low but RSI makes a HIGHER low (i.e. the first bullish divergence) — "get out of the trade immediately."**
  - In the example, doing this extended the trade to 1:3 R.
- Filters he adds:

  - Timeframe-agnostic claim: "every single trade setup works on a higher time frame and a lower time frame... your trades play out faster, that's the only difference" [4:57]-[5:13].
