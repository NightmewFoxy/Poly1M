# Why I Don't Trade On Monday
- id: YtkgdPZ9VE4 | views: 29000 | length: 370s
- market(s) shown: GER40 / "German 40" (DAX) on MT4 and TradingView; he mentions
  he pivoted away from NAS100 because it doesn't move well in London session
- timeframe(s) taught: 1h for analysis/entry ("one solid concrete trade on the 1
  hour time frame", [5:30]); explicitly argues **against** 1m/3m/5m entries;
  hourly chart used to show the weekend gap

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings:
  - **Heikin Ashi candles** instead of normal candles ([4:02]-[4:08]) —
    justification: "they are a better indication of when trend is going to
    continue in a direction".
  - A **New York midnight vertical line** indicator, purely to separate days
    visually ([1:22]-[1:29]).
  - "Removes all indicators" for higher-timeframe analysis ([1:15]-[1:21]).
  - Green/red boxes on his chart are **PD arrays** built from a customised **Gann
    box** tool ([4:17]-[4:26]) — no settings given, promised a future video.
- Setup/context required:
  - **Do not trade Monday.** Reason stated: only ~8 candles into the new week on
    his analysis timeframe, "not enough price information ... to fully form my
    bias" ([2:03]-[2:22], restated [3:23]-[3:30]).
  - Trade **with the trend** — "don't trade against the trend, you're trying to
    stop a freight train with your bare hands" ([2:31]-[2:38]).
  - Wants clean market structure before entering: "lower lows and lower highs,
    higher highs and higher lows" day in and day out ([3:32]-[3:44]).
  - Avoid low-volume consolidation: "I just wait until price actually starts
    getting some larger candles getting some momentum" ([3:51]-[3:59]).
- Entry trigger: on the 1h chart, **wait for price to pull back to his trend line,
  then enter on a Heikin Ashi candle with no wick on the bottom** (for longs)
  ([4:10]-[4:19], [5:26]-[5:37]): "every single one of these candles that started
  the move up with no wick on the bottom it was a good entry every single time".
- Stop loss: **below the previous swing** — the worked example was a **37 point
  stop loss** ([5:37]-[5:40]).
- Take profit / exit: two options stated:
  - Fixed **1:2 risk-to-reward** ([5:40]-[5:42]).
  - Or hold using Heikin Ashi: "you don't get out of a trade until you see the
    **opposite color candle**" — which in his example gave **1:6** ([5:43]-[5:50]).
- Filters he adds:
  - **Session:** he starts trading at **about 8:00 a.m. Central European Time**
    ([2:01]-[2:03]). Trades London session; picked GER40 because it's Europe-based
    and moves well in London ([0:53]-[1:13]).
  - **Day of week:** skip Monday; Monday's price action is used to build bias for
    Tuesday ([2:53]-[2:58]).
  - **Trade-count cap (cited statistic, not his own rule):** prop-firm data he
    cites says funded traders who get payouts 60%+ of months "only place about
    **15 trades per month maximum**"; more than that = overtrading ([4:26]-[4:57]).
