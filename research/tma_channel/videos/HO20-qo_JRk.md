# Become a Day Trading Pro: How to Identify and Trade Key Levels
- id: HO20-qo_JRk | views: 325000 | length: 322s
- market(s) shown: **AUDUSD** (daily, from Thursday 21 October 2021 onward) [0:39–0:42, 1:22–1:32]; **BTC** as a second example [4:36–4:53]
- timeframe(s) taught: **Daily for the level map, 1m or 5m for execution** — "if you're scalping on a much smaller time frame like the one minute or the five minute but you're looking at price structure" [3:03–3:12]

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
The video's thesis: scalpers mark reaction zones but never read **the actual price** at those zones, which is almost always a round "bank level".

- Indicators + exact settings: **None** — only rectangles/zones and horizontal lines. Explicit instruction: **"we're going to not use straight lines, we're going to use zones and boxes because it's not an exact science, but it's pretty damn close"** [1:04–1:16].
- Setup/context required: Build the level map first. **"take the price out of whatever you're trading out to a higher time frame"** [0:34–0:39] (he uses daily), then **"focus on these areas — the areas where price rejected and made a new trend"**, draw a **rectangle** at each and **extend it all the way out to the left** [0:44–1:22].
- Entry trigger: The actionable rule is stated as a **prohibition plus a sequence**:
  - **Do NOT enter at the level itself** — "another key mistake that people make is entering trades at these levels; you do not know definitively if it's going to go up or down from these levels, so getting in while price is here might keep you in an area of consolidation for more than you're comfortable with" [3:30–3:47].
  - **Instead: "wait until you're seeing definitive rejections on a higher time frame, then look for your higher highs and higher lows on your lower time frame like your five minute"** [3:47–3:56].
- Stop loss: not given as a formula — he only says this method "is really going to change where your take profits and where your stop losses are and even where your entries are" [1:01–1:04].
- Take profit: **The nearest bank level in the trade direction** — "the price targets that I want you to focus on when you're holding those scalps longer or going in a specific direction is look at which one it's closest to and how you can get into that trade into that zone" [3:15–3:30]. Purpose: "key levels like this are going to help you hold your trades longer" [4:53–4:58].
- Filters he adds:
  - **Bank / psychological levels are the real levels.** On AUDUSD he reads the actual prices off the zones: **0.7600** ("almost exactly", with the zone ~50 pips wide), **0.7300**, **0.7000** [1:46–2:15]. "the reason why price spikes from these areas is that they are known as **major bank levels** — banks are targeting these areas specifically because they are dumping a large sum of money… at these specific levels" [2:15–2:34].
  - **The 300-pip grid (the one hard number in the video):** "the difference between every one of these is **300 pips exactly** — so if you subtract 300 pips from 0.7000, what do you get? 0.6400" [2:40–2:52]. (Note: 0.7000 − 300 pips = 0.6700, not 0.6400; the transcript's spoken figures are internally inconsistent — the drawn line is "six four zero zero zero". **Frame-check required.**) He then draws that level and claims "more rejections, more support being shown at this specific level" [2:52–3:03].
  - **BTC round-number examples:** 35,000 / 47,000 / 18,000 / 34,000 / 30,000 [4:36–4:53].
  - **Higher-timeframe trend gate:** "we had a couple of fake outs as price kept pushing down but now we are clearly above that trend line, so I can comfortably say that AUDUSD on the daily time frame is on an uptrend **until price breaks this trend line and/or retests this level of known support and resistance**" [3:56–4:20].

## Vague / untestable / chart-pointed claims
- [0:44–1:04] "focus on these areas, the areas where price rejected and made a new trend, and only focus on these areas" — which rejections qualify is shown, not defined (no touch count, no minimum move after rejection, no lookback).
- [1:16–1:22] Rectangle **height** is never specified — he notes one range "is like 50 pips ish" [2:01–2:03] but gives no rule for where the box top/bottom go.
- [2:40–2:52] The 300-pip spacing claim is asserted from three AUDUSD levels only (0.76 / 0.73 / 0.70) and the arithmetic in the transcript does not close (see above). Treat the "grid" as an unproven pattern, not a rule.
- [3:47–3:52] "wait until you're seeing **definitive** rejections on a higher time frame" — "definitive" undefined; this is the actual entry gate.
- [3:56–4:20] The daily trend line is drawn on chart; "a couple of fake outs" and "clearly above that trend line" are eyeball judgements.
- [4:53–5:03] "rejections off of these key levels are monumentally more important than other levels" — asserted, not measured.
- **No stop-loss rule, no R multiple, no entry candle trigger, no session filter, no position sizing** anywhere in the video. It teaches a level map, not a trade plan.
