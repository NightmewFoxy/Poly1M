## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: **moving averages** in general ("as you guys know and by the name of the channel i like moving averages ... it shows you where you're seeing strength and resistance as well as seeing momentum") — **lengths and type never stated in this video** [9:37–9:58]. No other indicator is used; the entry is pure price action.
- Setup/context required: a run of consecutive candles in one direction — **it does not matter if it's 5, 7 or 12 consecutive candles, but the last three must be the same colour** [10:44–10:52]. He also frames the entry as occurring at a **moving-average cross or a rejection off the moving average** [10:02–10:07].
- Entry trigger: **three-line strike** — three consecutive same-colour candles followed by **one big engulfing candle in the opposite direction**; enter in the direction of the engulfing candle [10:19–11:01].
- Stop loss: **just above the big engulfing candle** for a short, **just below it** for a long [10:32–10:42, 11:01–11:04]. He calls this a "sniper entry" precisely because that stop is tight [11:23–11:44].
- Take profit: **20 pips, every trade** [11:04–11:08, 2:26–2:31]. In the worked example the 20-pip target filled in **2 hours 44 minutes** [11:51–11:57].
- Position sizing — the **20-pip challenge** ladder (the core mechanic):
  - Start with **$20** (or 20 EUR/GBP).
  - Each trade targets **20 pips**, sized so that 20 pips = **+30% of the current account balance**.
  - Level 1: balance $20 → profit goal **$6** → lot size **0.03** [4:44–4:56].
  - Level 2 profit goal **$8**, and so on; add each win to the balance before sizing the next trade [4:56–5:07].
  - By trades 15–17 the per-trade profit is **$200–$300**; **level 22 ≈ $1,400/trade; level 28 ≈ $7,000/trade** [5:48–6:12].
  - **30 consecutive winning trades takes $20 → $52,404** [6:12–6:28].
  - **Loss rule: if a trade hits the loss limit on the sheet you close the position and drop back one level** — e.g. failing at level 7 loses **$22** and returns you to level 6 [6:43–7:02].
  - Journaling column on the sheet: mark completed trades green, and log **why you entered, what timeframe, which indicators** [7:02–7:36].
- Filters he adds: none (no session, day or news filter). He explicitly labels the method **reckless and against every day-trading rule**, says **do this on demo**, and repeats it is not financial advice [1:17–1:49, 12:24–12:39].
