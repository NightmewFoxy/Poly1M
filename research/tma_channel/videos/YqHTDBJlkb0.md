# Best Divergence Trading Strategy
- id: YqHTDBJlkb0 | views: 172000 | length: 433s
- market(s) shown: AUDUSD and US30 ("two of my favorite assets to trade")
- timeframe(s) taught: 1h for the support/resistance markup ([1:23] "i've already drawn up my support and resistance levels on a one-hour time frame"); the entry timeframe is not restated in this video but the video is billed as an add-on to his 5-minute scalping video

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: **RSI** via the channel's "TMA divergence indicator" — described as "an rsi without the overbought and oversold areas", drawn as a single solid yellow line, with a **50 line** as the momentum threshold ([3:42]–[4:10]). RSI length is never stated. The indicator has toggles to plot regular bullish/bearish and hidden bullish/bearish divergences. He also accepts stochastic RSI as an alternative oscillator ([2:02]).
- Setup/context required: Trade **with the trend only**. First mark 1h horizontal support/resistance: "drawing horizontal lines across the peaks and the lows you usually want two above and two below the current price" ([1:31]–[1:38]). Confirm bullish structure = higher highs and higher lows (mirror for bearish).
- Entry trigger: A **hidden bullish divergence** — price makes a higher low while the RSI makes a lower low ([2:25]–[3:05]). Enter at the **close of the candle** on which the divergence completes: [4:41] "after that candle closed right there you could have entered in your trade". He adds that it is "especially" valid "if price action is on a trend line" ([3:12]).
- Stop loss: **below the bottom of that entry candle** ([4:55] "keeping your stop loss below the bottom of that candle").
- Take profit: **Two targets, both at prior 1h S/R levels** — first TP at the previous level of support/resistance, second TP at the next one ([4:47]–[4:55]). The worked example is stated as **1:3 R, +46 pips total** ([4:59]–[5:04]).
- Filters he adds:
  - Direction must match the trend; hidden divergences are used only as trend-continuation signals.
  - **Exit rule / trade management**: switch the indicator to show only **regular bearish divergences** (price higher high, RSI lower high) and use the first one as the exit — [5:57]–[6:18] "that is a regular divergence so what you can do is go into the settings and only look for bearish divergences because that's going to be your exit point for your trade". Useful when trailing a long-term position.
  - Prior S/R levels are treated as price magnets ([6:35]–[6:39]).
  - No session, day-of-week or news filter in this video.

## Vague / untestable / chart-pointed claims
- [1:31]–[1:38] "two above and two below the current price" S/R markup — which peaks/lows qualify is left to eye; the levels are the take-profit rule, so the TP is discretionary.
- [3:05]–[3:09] "you can put trend lines up to see it clearly" — divergence confirmation by hand-drawn oscillator trendlines.

- RSI length, and any requirement for the RSI pivots to be beyond a threshold, are absent (contrast GGD9kjgHViI, where he insists divergence pivots must be beyond 70/30 — this video's indicator deliberately removes those bands).
- [6:03] Divergence signals come from a proprietary indicator whose detection logic is not specified; he elsewhere notes it does not print every time.
