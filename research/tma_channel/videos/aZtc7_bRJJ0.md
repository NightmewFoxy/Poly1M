## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: none. Only a **Fibonacci retracement** is invoked
  (see entry trigger).
- Setup/context required (seasonal filter — the whole thesis):
  - **Trade September through November**; summer is low volume, low volatility
    ([0:00]-[0:08], [1:40]-[1:52]).
  - Trend must exist: "ride trends, don't fight them ... if gold is trending, ride
    the trend. If EuroUSD is breaking down, follow it down" ([4:11]-[4:29]).
- Entry trigger: **limit order in the Fibonacci "gold zone" — between the 50 and
  the 0.618 retracement** ([4:31]-[4:54]): "set up limit orders during this time.
  Trends continue from September through November. So, if price retraces to the
  Fibonacci gold zone, set a limit order between the 50 and the 618."
  He credits this to "my Fibonacci scalping strategy" (taught elsewhere).
- Stop loss: not stated in this video.
- Take profit: not stated in this video.
- Filters he adds:
  - **Session:** "focus on liquidity sessions. **London open and New York open**
    are absolute gold mines during this season and that's where the volume is"
    ([3:40]-[3:52]). No clock times or timezone given.
  - **Timeframe stacking:** "stack your setups on 15minute, 1 hour, or even 4hour
    charts" ([4:05]-[4:11]).
  - **News blackout rules** ([5:58]-[8:12]) — the most numeric content in the video:
    - **NFP (non-farm payrolls): first Friday of every month.** Rule: "mark the
      calendar, expect volatility, and either **reduce your lot size or sit it out
      for the first 15 minutes after release**" ([6:24]-[6:47]).
    - **CPI: released around mid-month.** Rule: "either trade smaller or stay out
      until the dust settles" ([6:47]-[7:11]).
    - **FOMC: Fed meetings usually fall at the end of the month.** Rule: "be
      cautious trading right into them. Let the market digest the Fed's tone before
      making your move" ([7:11]-[7:33]).
    - **Earnings season, October especially** — if you trade S&P 500 / NASDAQ,
      tech earnings (Tesla, Apple, Microsoft, Amazon) can make "indices move like
      crypto" ([7:33]-[8:01]).
    - Governing principle: "During these events, **your strategy does not change.
      Your risk management does.** Don't size up just because the market is moving
      fast" ([8:03]-[8:16]).
  - **Journal every single trade** through the season to build a playbook
    ([4:56]-[5:14]).
