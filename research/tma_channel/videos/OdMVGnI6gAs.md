## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings:
  - **RSI**: colour yellow, **upper band and lower band both changed to 50**, kept as a **solid line**, **length 14** (standard input) [2:00-2:30]
  - Three **smoothed moving averages: 21, 50, 200** — used only "so I can get their idea of what the price is doing, if it's trending up or trending down" [2:30-2:40]
  - He calls the whole thing "strictly momentum trading" [1:57-2:00]
- Setup/context required: draw horizontal support/resistance by "drawing a horizontal line across multiple intersecting points", taking only the **big rejection areas** [1:25-1:42]. He notes his own US30 chart back to April 16 needed only **three lines** [1:42-1:50]. Then wait for price to return and **retest** a level a second time — the double top / double bottom.
- Entry trigger (the actual novelty of the video): the second touch must be **flat/level with the first on price**, while the **RSI prints a divergent (non-level) pair of swings** at the same two points:
  - **Double bottom → long**: the two price lows are "completely flat and parallel", but the RSI shows "a low point here and a higher low here, creating a divergence... meaning that the momentum has diminished and we should see a pump to the upside" [2:46-3:41]
  - **Double top → short**: the two price highs are "perfectly horizontal, on the same exact level" but the RSI's second high is lower — "drawing a trend line across that you can see it is not horizontal, meaning that that should go on the downside" [3:57-4:24]
  - Test method he gives: use the trendline tool across the two price swings and across the two RSI swings and compare — one is level, the other is not [3:29-3:36, 4:14-4:23]
  - Enter on the **rejection candle** off the level (in the long example, "this big, huge green candle") [3:03-3:08, 3:41-3:49]
- Stop loss: **below the bottom of the wick** of the rejection candle for longs; **above the wicks** for shorts [3:49-3:53, 4:24-4:27].
- Take profit: **1:2 risk-to-reward** — "you're looking for about a 1 to 2 risk reward ratio" [3:53-3:55], repeated for the short example [4:27-4:30]. Worked results: ~200 points off the long, ~140 points off the short [3:55-4:01, 4:27-4:34].
- Filters he adds: higher timeframe only ("it only works on higher time frames... on higher time frames you have a clear indication of actual support and resistance levels") [4:30-4:42]; the video's stated purpose is solving the "is it a triple top?" problem — the RSI divergence is what tells you the second retest will actually hold [0:20-0:37].
