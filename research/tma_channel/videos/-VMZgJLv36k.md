- Filters he adds: Order block identification signature (the closest thing to a mechanical rule):
  1. Very strong rejections off a specific price zone
  2. With either long wicks or very big candles ("big fat candle", "huge wicks")
  3. Repeated rejection off the SAME zone (multiple touches)
  4. Double bottoms are "usually a nice indication of order blocks" [4:33]-[4:39]
  5. A prior all-time high / previous high that broke and cascaded down becomes "a new order block for sellers" [3:28]-[3:41]
  6. Order blocks exist at both large scale (institutional) and small scale
  Summary he gives himself at [6:04]-[6:14]: "large financial institutions and billionaires buy a bunch, big wicks, big candles, look at a higher time frame and look left".
