## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
A live trade post-mortem. The trailing rules are the extractable content.

- Indicators + exact settings: **Fibonacci retracement** — he names the **0.382 retracement level** as his original take-profit target ("this is the 382 retracement level of that bigger down move", [2:23]) and the **"fib gold zone"** as the extended target he moved to ([2:37]). Also a **21 moving average on the 1-minute** mentioned in passing ([4:30]) — type unstated.
- Setup/context required: price **broke an area**, and he waited for a **break and retest** of it — "the only thing I could do was wait for a break and a retest, and then you have a swing low to get your stop loss at" ([0:25]).
- Entry trigger: a **long lower wick followed by a large engulfing candle**; entered **on the close of that candle** ([0:32]–[0:44]).
- Stop loss (initial): at the **swing low** created by the break-and-retest ([0:31]).
- Trailing stop rules — three distinct stages, all stated:
  1. Early: "**every time it went up and created a low I moved my stop loss to just below that — like a couple of points, not one point but a couple of points**" ([1:10]).
  2. Once in profit: move the stop **into profit** = risk-free trade ([2:04]–[2:12]).
  3. Late/parabolic: "**I moved my stop loss to 50 of that candle**" — i.e. to the **50% midpoint of each new large engulfing candle**, rolled forward as each one prints ([2:49], [3:19]–[3:39]).
- Take profit: originally the **0.382 fib retracement (≈34,000)**; he then **moved the target up to 30,500 / the fib gold zone** as momentum built ([2:28]–[2:41]). In the end the trailing stop took him out before either.
- Result stated: entry **3346**, exited on trailing stop at **34.04** after price topped at **34.29** and rejected; **+$336** on the trade, which took his funded challenge account to **+$38 overall** ([3:40]–[4:27]).
- Filters he adds: none stated. He notes the R:R was poor going in — "**horrible risk to reward, but with the potential to go to a fib retracement level, so I went for it**" ([0:04]).
