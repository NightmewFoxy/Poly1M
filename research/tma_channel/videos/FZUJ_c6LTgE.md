# 10 Pips a Day is EASY with this Strategy
- id: FZUJ_c6LTgE | views: 413000 | length: 442s
- market(s) shown: AUDUSD ("my preferred 4X pair to trade")
- timeframe(s) taught: 5m or 15m for the scalp ([6:18] "if you're trading on the 5 minute or the 15-minute in your scalping"); session windows shown on chart

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: **21, 50 and 200 SMOOTHED moving averages** (stated explicitly: [0:32] "the 21, the 50, and the 200 smooth, moving averages"). RSI is added only for the counter-trend variant ("also having your RSI up... you should be also trying to spot an RSI Divergence" [4:38]–[4:46]) — RSI length/levels unstated. A session-marking indicator is used to shade the trading window.
- Setup/context required: Three named setups, all keyed off the 200 SMMA:
  1. Price **above** the 200 SMMA, comes down to retest it → long.
  2. Price **below** the 200 SMMA, comes up to retest it → short.
  3. Price trading **between** the moving averages with a visible slope → scalp between the 50 and the 200.
  [0:57]–[1:26] "if we are above it and we come down to retest, I go for long positions, if we are below it, and come up to retest, I go for short positions... if the price is trading between the moving averages, and I start seeing a slope, I will scalp between the 50 and the 200."
- Entry trigger: **An engulfing candle at the retest; enter at the close of that candle.** [2:57] "this candle engulfed this candle, so this would be my entry point." [3:32] "enter in on the close of that candle." For the counter-trend variant: wait for price to break the 21 and/or the 50 and retest it, moving averages start curving, then enter on the engulfing candle at the retest ([4:12]–[5:17]).
- Stop loss: **10 pips.** [3:45] "I'm risking 10 tips to get 10 tips because if the price goes back ten Pips, I know I was wrong on my entry."
- Take profit: **10 pips, 1:1 risk-to-reward.** [3:40] "with this tentative strategy I'm not looking for anything more than one to one risk-to-reward ratio." Both example trades hit +10 pips within ~1h (one counter-trend trade took ~3h).
- Filters he adds:
  - **Trade a defined session**: London or New York, marked with a session indicator; his own example opens the London session **at 9 a.m. his local time** ([2:41] "The market is going to open up right here at 9 a.m. For me, London session") — timezone not stated beyond "for me".
  - Prefer with-trend entries; counter-trend takes longer and is not advocated ([3:56], [5:20]).
  - If price is very far from the 200 SMMA, do not take a trend-continuation trade; wait for the 21/50 break-and-retest instead ([4:03]–[4:20]).
  - **Risk sizing**: 1 standard lot = $10/pip, so a 10-pip stop = $100 risk; at 1% risk that implies a $10,000 account ([5:37]–[5:58]). To risk 2%, take **two** trades of 10 pips rather than widening the stop ([5:58]–[6:12]).
