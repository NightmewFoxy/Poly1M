# Ichimoku Cloud: What It Means and How to Use It
- id: H9eILCI5nJo | views: 234000 | length: 534s
- market(s) shown: **GBPUSD** (the live trade he reviews) [4:51–4:56, 6:47–6:50]; an unnamed chart for the general cloud explanation
- timeframe(s) taught: **Daily → 1h → 15m or 5m** as a top-down stack — "draw horizontal lines across areas of support and resistance… then you can take it down to the one hour time frame and get some minor ones… then you can take it down to the 15 minute or the five minute and then toss on the Ichimoku cloud" [6:44–7:32]

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: **Ichimoku Cloud** — TradingView's built-in, found by searching "ichimoku", **"the first one under Technicals"** [0:56–1:03]. Configuration change he prescribes: **remove the lagging span (the green line)** [1:06–1:11], leaving conversion line, baseline, leading span A, leading span B. Periods he states (i.e. TradingView defaults, unchanged):
  - **Conversion line (blue) = 9-period average** [1:38–1:43]
  - **Baseline (red) = 26-period average** [1:46–1:49]
  - **Span B = 52-period average** [2:04–2:09]
  - **Span A = (baseline + conversion line) / 2** [2:11–2:18]
  - **The cloud is displaced 25 bars forward** — "the cloud leads the price action by 25 bars… the current price is right here and 25 bars ahead is where this cloud ends" [2:36–2:49]
- Setup/context required: A **top-down S/R map** built first: on the higher timeframe draw horizontals at "where the price has rejected the most — those are going to be your targets" [6:47–7:03]; add minor levels from the 1h in a **neutral colour, one above and one below current price** [7:06–7:20]. Then apply the cloud on 15m/5m.
- Entry trigger: One explicit long-side rule: **"if the trend is going down and the price is breaking through the cloud and dancing in it, wait till it's comfortably trading above with the conversion line, the baseline and the cloud all below the price — then you can target the next support and resistance zone"** [8:04–8:19]. The mirror bearish condition is described as the setup that produced a sustained move: **"the cloud turned red, the baseline and the conversion line were both below the cloud, and it continued to cascade down"** [6:07–6:16]; also "as the conversion line and the baseline drop below the cloud pushing the price down you're getting that nice bearish momentum" [3:54–4:05].
- Stop loss: No placement formula. He gives a **philosophy** instead: "stop losses are not… 'this is how much I'm willing to lose', it's an indication of **my analysis was wrong** — at what point am I so wrong that the price is not going to respect that and completely invalidate all of my analysis" [5:35–5:57]. In the reviewed trade the actual numbers were **TP 1.3570, SL 1.3530** [5:13–5:19] (transcript renders them as "35 700" / "35 300"), i.e. a ~40-pip stop on GBPUSD.
- Take profit: **The next support/resistance zone, found by looking left** — "what you can do for your price targets is actually just look left to see where that Ichimoku cloud had that rejection area; that is the next layer of previous support" [4:25–4:35]. He also flags **round-number "bank levels"**: "keeping in mind this huge bank level of an even number, 1.35" [7:52–7:58].
- Filters he adds:
  - **Cloud thickness = barrier strength.** "think of this cloud as a barrier — the thicker the cloud the stronger the barrier, the thinner the cloud the easier it is to go through" [2:49–2:58]; "thin cloud means less momentum, thicker cloud means more momentum" [3:49–3:54].
  - **Cloud slope as trend confirmation:** "as the cloud leads it starts to slope downwards giving you a heavier indication that the trend will continue down" [4:09–4:18].
  - **Do NOT counter-trend trade** — the whole trade review is a cautionary tale: "don't get into a counter trend trade if all of these things are pushing the price down" [7:58–8:04]; "so much easier to trade with the trend" [6:23–6:28].
  - **Risk cap:** "I only risked one percent on the trade" [5:24–5:28].
