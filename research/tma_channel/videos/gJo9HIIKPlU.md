In the actual execution the trigger was a **trap candle followed by a huge bearish engulfing candle** — [9:54]–[10:04] "the next candle that formed was this huge engulfing candle at this point right here i entered three positions." He then **scaled in with two more shorts** when price rallied back into a marked S/R zone and started rejecting off it ([10:06]–[10:19]).
- Stop loss: **not stated anywhere in this video.** He criticises other traders' stops as "way too tight" ([9:01], [10:36]) but never gives his own placement or size.

Earlier on the live stream he framed three sequential targets = the **21, 50 and 100 moving averages** ([8:09]–[8:28]: "this is your first take profit this is your second take profit this is your third take profit... what you can do is target these moving averages").
