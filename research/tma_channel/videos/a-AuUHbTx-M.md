- Filters he adds (the entire content of the video):
  - **Trade only while US banks are open: 09:00–17:00 Eastern (UTC−4)**
    ([1:14]-[1:18], [2:09]-[2:23]) — "what time are banks in New York open 9:00
    a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Eastern Standard Time also known as UTC minus 4".
  - **Avoid the first hour to hour-and-a-half of the New York open (08:00–09:00 /
    up to ~09:30)** — "there is a lot of this wish-wash he movement this is where
    people get screwed because price is moving up and down and trying to collect
    liquidity before deciding on a direction for the day ... I want you to
    completely avoid that" ([2:33]-[2:56]).
  - **Narrowed window: trade a maximum of 3 hours per day, 11:00 → 14:00 New York
    time (Eastern, UTC−4)** ([2:26]-[3:11]) — "I want you to start trading at about
    11:00 a.m. New York time Eastern Standard Time UTC minus 4 and I want you to
    focus on the next 3 hours so till about 2 p.m. or military time 1400".
  - Rationale for the window: keeps you out of flat Tokyo session and out of
    pre-NY-open London where movement is smaller, and out of the open's whipsaw
    ([3:11]-[3:32]).
  - Explicit observation used as justification: the big price spike on his chart
    "started at about **8:00 a.m.** and ended at about **3:00 p.m.**"
    ([1:04]-[1:14]).
