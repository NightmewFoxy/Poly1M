## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
This is his most-viewed video and the source of the fib levels used across the rest of the channel.

- Indicators + exact settings: **Fibonacci retracement tool**. Levels named explicitly:
  - **0.5 and 0.618 = the "Fibonacci gold zone"** — the entry area ([1:13]–[1:20]).
  - **0.382** — mentioned as a shallower retracement that occurs on strong/fast moves ([2:50]).
  - **−0.382 = take profit 2**; **−0.618 = take profit 3**; **−1.618 = the "holy crap" extension, for swing trading** ([2:18]–[2:35]).
  - He tells the viewer to copy his tool settings off the screen: "go into the settings and adjust all your settings to look exactly like mine, take a screenshot, save it for future reference" ([0:39]) — **the actual settings are never spoken**.
- Setup/context required: read **market structure** first — lower highs and lower lows. **Once market structure fails to make another lower low, you are looking at the opposite-direction trend** ([0:49]–[1:02]).
- How to draw the fib (this is stated precisely): **anchor from the point where it had its lowest, to the point where it failed to continue market structure** ([1:05]–[1:14]).
- Entry trigger: price retraces into the **0.5–0.618 gold zone**, PLUS a **price-action reversal confirmation** — "you don't want to enter blindly, you want to look for some sort of price action reversal at that point" ([1:23]). In his example the confirmation was a **double bottom** (two touches, flat line) **plus an RSI divergence** (price low then equal/lower low, RSI a higher low) ([1:41]–[1:53]). Enter once it rejects off that area ([1:53]).
- Stop loss: **none stated.**
- Take profit: laddered off the fib extensions —
  - **TP1 = the prior swing high** ("you are almost guaranteed to reach back to that higher high that it was at… the likelihood of price making a double top and then consolidating is very high if the momentum is high", [2:02]–[2:18]);
  - **TP2 = −0.382**; **TP3 = −0.618**; **−1.618** only in exceptional/swing cases ([2:21]–[2:35]).
- Filters he adds: before targeting a deep extension, **look left for prior price targets in that range** — "definitely look left and see if there are any price targets in that range; if they are then it's most likely that it will continue" ([2:37]). RSI divergence used as confluence at the gold zone. Fibs are more reliable on higher timeframes.
