## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: three moving averages — "the 21 / the 50 / the 200" [0:44-0:46]. Type not stated in this video (he calls them just "moving averages"; other videos of his call the same trio smoothed/SMMA).
- Setup/context required: an ANTI-rule video. The stated premise: do NOT enter at the moment two MAs cross. "price tends to reverse after a moving average crossover" [1:10-1:12]. Trade only within the band price occupies between the 21, 50 and 200.
- Entry trigger: after a crossover has already happened, "wait for the price to pull back and then reject off of the moving averages going with the trend" [2:31-2:38]. Staged version: "crossing and closing above [the 21] targeting the 50; crossing and closing above [the 50] targeting the 200" [2:13-2:22] — i.e. entry requires a candle CLOSE above the MA, not just a touch.
- Stop loss: not stated. He only criticises the crossover trader's "very tight" stop [1:06-1:08]; gives no replacement placement rule.
- Take profit: next moving average up the ladder — close above 21 → target the 50; close above 50 → target the 200 [2:16-2:22].
