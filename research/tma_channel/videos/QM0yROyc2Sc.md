## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: none.
- Setup/context required: none — this is a seasonality/market-conditions talk, not
  a strategy video.
- Entry trigger: none given.
- Stop loss: none given.
- Take profit: none given.
- Filters he adds — the only actionable rules, all calendar-based:
  - **Do not day trade in December** unless you have specifically done or
    backtested December before ([5:31]-[5:39]): "unless you have done December
    before successfully or you've practiced it or you've back tested it I would
    recommend not trading in December."
  - **If you do trade December, trade it on a demo account** ([7:54]-[8:07]).
  - **Breakout traders specifically are warned off** ([1:10]-[1:25]): low volume
    means "your strategy ... may not work as
    anticipated especially if you're a breakout Trader and you're looking for
    breakouts of specific support and resistance zones — the volume won't be there
    to take advantage of that breakout."
  - **Expect volatility spikes around Q4 earnings reports** and around
    tax-loss-harvesting flows ([3:11]-[3:25], [5:12]-[5:28]) — he characterises
    them as brief: "it just does this for 15 minutes and then it goes back to
    normal."
  - **Santa Claus rally:** "certain assets will rally right after Christmas ...
    **between Christmas and just after New Year's** you will see some assets just
    spike massively, lots of volume, lots of movement" ([4:25]-[4:42]) — named but
    never turned into an entry rule.
- Stated *causes* of the December regime (background, not rules) ([0:07]-[1:10]):
  vacation days concentrated in December, national bank holidays closing banks,
  and most FX/day-trading flow routing through those banks → **lower than normal
  volume**.
- **Tax loss harvesting** explained at [1:28]-[3:11]: sell the losing asset before
  year end to book the loss on paper, offset it against the year's gains (his
  example: $100k profit offset by a $60k Facebook loss), then buy the asset back.
  Explicitly flagged "not a recommendation" and "there are different legalities."
