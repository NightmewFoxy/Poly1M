## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: TradingView built-in **"All Candlestick Patterns"** indicator (search "all patterns" under Technicals). Exact configuration given [3:53]-[4:37]:
  - **Detect trend based on: "No detection"** — explicitly *not* the 50 and *not* the 200 MA option.
  - **Pattern types: Bullish** only.
  - Tick only these patterns: **Dragonfly Doji, Engulfing, Hammer, Morning Star, Morning Doji Star, Piercing, Three White Soldiers**. "Don't tick any of the other ones."
  - Also references Williams Fractals as the concept behind pivot marking [1:02]-[1:13] (no settings given).
- Setup/context required: **trade only trend continuation** — "if you only take trend continuation trades you will be in good hands... the statistics show that price will continue its trend 60 percent of the time" [0:25]-[0:46]. For the bullish patterns in this video, price must be in an uptrend, i.e. making **higher highs and higher lows** [5:01]-[5:06], [5:13]-[5:15].
- Entry trigger: **when the candle closes**, the indicator prints the pattern name/arrow; enter long on a printed bullish pattern while price is in an uptrend [4:37]-[5:06]. His own preference among them:
  - Favourite = **bullish engulfing**, and specifically he wants it in the three-line-strike shape: **three candles of the opposite (bearish) direction followed by one engulfing candle in the trend direction** [2:09]-[2:25].
  - He "primarily trades" the **Morning Star, Morning Doji Star and the three line strike** [3:02]-[3:32].
  - He dislikes the **inverted hammer** "because of the long wick on top" [1:36]-[1:43].
  - He does **not** trade the **piercing line** (daily/swing pattern) [2:26]-[2:36].
- Stop loss: **not stated anywhere in this video.**
- Take profit: **"shoot for your 10 pips like you learned in my previous video"** — take the 10 pips once price starts making higher highs and higher lows [5:06]-[5:18]. (Cross-reference: the 10-pip target is the same figure used in yHg3nqmOxqE, where it is paired with a 10-pip stop / 20-pip target.)
- Filters he adds:
  - Uptrend / trend-continuation only (bullish patterns are not to be taken against the trend).
  - **Market-structure filter to cut losers:** "if you couple that with market structure and looking for higher highs and higher lows you can see that when price rejects off of specific areas you can remove half of these signals that made you lose" [5:37]-[5:52].
  - Pattern-definition notes given verbatim from Investopedia for the piercing line [2:36]-[2:56]: preceded by a downtrend, price gaps lower to begin the second day, and the second candle must close **above the midpoint of the first candle**.
  - Hammer directional read: "you got to think about the hammer and then the arm that swings it — depending on what the next candle is, that's usually the direction that the hammer is going to go" [1:41]-[1:54].
  - Dragonfly doji definition: opens and closes at the exact same price with a long lower wick [1:54]-[2:07].
