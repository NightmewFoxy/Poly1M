# 10 Minute Divergence Master Class
- id: 3APFZa1AQNw | views: 33000 | length: 634s
- market(s) shown: BTC (1h and daily), German 40 / DAX (15m)
- timeframe(s) taught: multi — 15m, 1h, daily. Core claim is that higher-timeframe divergences dominate lower ones.

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
This is the channel's most substantive RSI/divergence video and contains his self-described "secret sauce".
- Indicators + exact settings: **RSI on DEFAULT settings** — he says so explicitly and names the bands: **"these are the default settings and you can see right here that the top of the range is 70 and the bottom of the range is 30"** [0:39]-[0:50]. (Length is not spoken but "default" = 14.) The 70/30 band is repeatedly called "the 70/30 range".
- Setup/context required — five rules, presented as five common mistakes:
  1. **RSI is a MOMENTUM indicator, not an overbought/oversold oscillator. Invert the retail rule: when RSI is in the HIGH (above-70) area you should be looking for BUYS; when it is in the LOW (below-30) area you should be looking for SELLS.** "when you're in this area you should be looking for sells and when you're in this area you should be looking for buys, I know that's contrary to popular belief" [1:24]-[1:39]. (Note: his phrasing at [1:28] is inverted relative to the rest of the passage; from the Bitcoin example that follows — shorting at RSI 70 got people liquidated as BTC ran 77k -> 94k — the intended rule is clearly: above 70 = bullish momentum persists, don't short it.) Blunt version: **"if you're guilty of selling when RSI is at 70 and buying when RSI is at 30, please stop right now; you need other confluences in order to get into a trade"** [3:10]-[3:24].
  2. **Analyse the RSI itself like price**: it is effectively a line chart, so **draw trend lines ON the RSI**. RSI uptrends/downtrends and RSI trend-line BREAKS are tradeable information; a downtrend break while still above 70 signals high volatility/momentum [3:24]-[5:13].
  3. **Divergence definition**: a discrepancy between price and RSI — price makes higher highs while RSI makes lower highs -> look for the trend to reverse [5:13]-[5:36].
  4. **Higher-timeframe divergences always outweigh lower-timeframe divergences.** "if you're looking at a divergence on the five minute hoping to get a huge swing trade and the market to reverse, you're wrong; if you see a divergence on the 1 hour time frame that's going to be a swing trade banger" [5:36]-[5:59].
  5. **Hidden divergences (trend continuation) always outweigh regular divergences** — "the trend is your friend" [8:40]-[9:01], [9:56]-[10:08].
- Entry trigger — **the "secret sauce" filter for choosing WHICH divergence to trade** [6:43]-[7:53]:
  - On a series of successive divergences, check where the RSI high sits relative to the 70/30 range.
  - Divergences whose RSI high is still **ABOVE the 70/30 range** = momentum still bullish -> **do not take them** (in the BTC example, taking those "you would have been screwed").
  - **The tradeable one is the divergence where the RSI high drops back INSIDE the 70/30 range** — "price was above the 7030 range and then the next one was within the 7030 range, meaning a huge loss of momentum, and when things lose momentum they tend to reverse" [7:26]-[7:43].
  - Entry in the example: short **on the doji candle** at that final in-range divergence, which caught the exact BTC top [7:43]-[7:57].
- Stop loss: **not stated anywhere in this video.**
- Take profit: **a three-tier scale-out using key levels to the left as S/R** — take some off at TP1, more at TP2, "with your overall goal at take profit three" [8:00]-[8:14]. No R multiples given; the BTC example is quoted as a 32,000-point short.
- Filters he adds:
  - Divergence definitions given precisely:
    - **Regular (reversal)**: in an uptrend, price higher HIGHS vs RSI lower highs [9:01]-[9:07].
    - **Hidden (continuation)**: in an uptrend, price higher LOWS vs RSI LOWER low [9:07]-[9:39]. He walks through pivots where price made a higher low and RSI also made a higher low = NOT a hidden divergence; only the one where RSI made a lower low counts.
    - **Conflict resolution: a hidden bullish divergence at the lows OVERRIDES a regular bearish divergence at the highs** [9:39]-[9:56] — the single most actionable rule in the video.
  - Respect the trend, "especially when the RSI is in this range as it's a momentum indicator and price is most likely to continue in the trend" [9:56]-[10:08].
  - No sessions, times, timezone, days or news filter anywhere.
