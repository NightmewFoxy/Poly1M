# Stop Hunts EXPLAINED: How to Beat Market Manipulation

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
Three manipulation patterns, each with a stated detection method.

**1. Stop hunt**
- Setup/context: an obvious, widely-watched support (or resistance) level with retail stops clustered just beyond it. Example shown: gold 15m, where "price [drove] below yesterday's previous low, cleared all the stops, and then rebounded" ([2:14]).
- Entry trigger: **do not chase the break — wait; if price breaks the level and quickly comes back, that is the signal it was a liquidity grab** ([2:29]). He adds a waiting rule: "wait for a few more candles" ([3:19]).

**2. False breakout**
- Detection step 1: **volume** — "real breakouts are usually accompanied by sustained volume, not just one spike. If the volume fades immediately, it's probably fake" ([4:16]).
- Detection step 2: **Fibonacci retracement** — "a true breakout usually comes after a healthy retracement and build-up. If price just shoots straight into resistance and breaks out, chances are it is fake" ([4:32]).

**3. Liquidity sweep** (the one with the most specified trigger)
- Setup/context: price pushes aggressively into an area everyone is watching (e.g. **the previous day's high**), pokes just above the previous structure, collects the breakout buys, then reverses ([5:01]–[5:20]).
- Indicators + exact settings: **RSI** — length and levels never stated. The condition is **bearish divergence: price makes a higher high, RSI makes a lower high** ([5:43]). Explicit timeframe filter: "on the lower timeframes, divergences can be noisy. So avoid the 1 and 3 minutes. 15 minutes, 1 hour and daily deviations matter more" ([5:52]).
- Entry trigger: **"if you have an aggressive engulfing candle right after a breakout and a divergence, that's going to be your setup"** ([6:07]). i.e. structure break + close above prior high + RSI divergence + engulfing candle against the break.
- Stop loss: **none stated.**
- Take profit: **none stated.**

**His stated 3-step process ([6:27]–[6:58]):**
1. Mark key levels — **yesterday's highs and lows**, obvious S/R zones.
2. Watch how price reacts at those levels — "break it and hold it or break it back?"
3. Add indicators — RSI divergence or the Fibonacci retracement tool — to confirm real move vs trap.

- Filters he adds: avoid 1m/3m for divergence; wait several candles after the sweep before entering; a wick-out of a trade is itself read as confirmation of manipulation ([7:39]).
