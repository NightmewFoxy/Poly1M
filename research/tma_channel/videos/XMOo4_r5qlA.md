## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
Title says 3, he says "four examples" [0:06] and delivers four candle triggers. Common template stated at the end: **"you want the candle formation to form, you want the candle to close, you want to get in on your trade"** [2:25–2:29].

- Indicators + exact settings: none, except **Heikin Ashi candles** as a chart type (no settings). No MA, no oscillator.
- Trigger 1 — **Engulfing candle** [0:24–0:52]:
  - Setup: an uptrend (for the bullish version).
  - Rule: one bullish candle engulfs the previous bearish candle — "this candle body was completely engulfed and closed higher than the opening price of this candle"; "if it's a little bit or a lot of bit it doesn't matter."
  - Entry: that candle. Stop/target not given for this one on its own.
- Trigger 2 — **Three line strike** (his stated favourite) [0:55–1:15]:
  - Rule: **three bearish candles followed by one bullish engulfing candle** (mirror for bearish).
  - **Entry at the close of the [strike] candle, stop loss below.**
  - Take profit not stated in this video.
- Trigger 3 — **Doji** [1:16–1:33]:
  - Rule: doji = "where the open and close is the exact same price". **Wait for the next candle**; if the next candle is bullish, enter long.
  - **Stop loss below the swing. Target 1 : 2.**
- Trigger 4 — **Heikin Ashi color-change entry** [1:33–2:18]:
  - Setup: strong prior momentum (his example: heavy bearish momentum down).
  - Loss-of-momentum signal: a Heikin Ashi candle **with a wick on both the top and the bottom**.
  - Then the color changes to the opposite momentum.
  - **Entry = the first candle with no wick on the bottom (for a bullish move) / no wick on the top (for a bearish move).**
  - **Stop loss below the swing. Target 1 : 2.**
  - Re-entry allowed after a stop-out: "you get stopped out, you get another entry" — he shows 2 trades, 1 loss 1 win, net **+1%** at 1:2 [2:11–2:18].
- Filters he adds: none — no session, no news, no trend filter beyond "in an uptrend" for the engulfing example.
