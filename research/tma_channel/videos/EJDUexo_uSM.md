## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
This is a **market-structure mapping method** plus one concrete entry recipe bolted on at the end.

- Indicators + exact settings: **None for the structure method** — only horizontal lines. For entries: **Fibonacci retracement**, levels **0.5 and 0.618** [5:55–6:07]. For consolidation trading (which he discourages): **Gann box tool** — "here are the settings to my Gann box" [7:13–7:19] — **settings shown on screen only, never spoken.**
- Setup/context required: A structure map built by this procedure:
  1. **When a higher high forms, draw a horizontal line across it** [1:09–1:15].
  2. **Until price breaks above that higher high, no higher low has been formed** [1:36–1:41]. Once price breaks above, the **lowest point of the pullback preceding the break** is the confirmed higher low [1:41–1:56].
  3. **Delete every intermediate swing** — "you remove these ones that are considered the noise in between the true market structure" [1:41–1:47]; "you will identify the lowest one and ignore the ones that are just adding more noise" [2:14–2:20].
  4. Bearish is the mirror: identify lows; once price breaks a previous low and retraces, those are the new lows, and **"the highest point between those two lows is going to be your lower high"** [3:36–3:50].
- Entry trigger: **Limit orders in the 0.5–0.618 Fib zone.** "once you've identified a strong pullback after a break of a higher high, from the low to the high, I want you to draw a Fibonacci retracement tool… put limit orders in anywhere from the 0.5 to the 0.618" [5:41–6:01]. Note the anchor here is **low → high of the confirmed structure leg**, not "the impulse that broke structure" phrasing used in CpSLTA9BXjc.
- Stop loss: **At the previous higher low** (in an uptrend) [6:05–6:10].
- Take profit: **At the higher high** [6:10–6:12]. Rationale given: "worst case scenario, market will reverse on us breaking that previous structure of the higher low, and/or consolidate creating a double top" [6:12–6:22].
- Filters he adds:
  - **Candle CLOSE required to break structure** — "wicks below previous structure are again just noise; you want a candle closure below previous structure to determine if price is going to change direction" [2:47–2:58]; restated [4:20–4:28] and [1:36].
  - **Patience filter on retracements:** ignore one- or two-candle reversals; "be a little bit more patient and wait for two or three candles to form" [4:01–4:13].
  - **Broken structure ⇒ assume CONSOLIDATION, not reversal** — "what I want you to automatically assume is not huge reversal… what I want you to assume is consolidation, that because momentum stopped, price is most likely to consolidate, not fully reverse" [5:00–5:14].
  - **No-trade zone:** "if you want to continue trading the trend because you're a trend trader, you do not trade this while it is in this box" [5:19–5:26]; "you do not trade until the price breaks out of this range" [7:00–7:05].
  - **Optional consolidation trade (explicitly discouraged, twice):** draw a Gann box from the range high to the range low; **"you will only buy at the bottom of this range and sell at the top of this range"** [7:17–7:36]. "This is not fun… these areas kind of make me want to puke. I avoid them" [7:55–8:08].
