## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: **Fibonacci retracement + Fibonacci extension**, plus **volume** (standard volume histogram; no settings, no threshold given). Retracement levels named: **38.2%, 50%, 61.8%** [0:55–1:02]. Extension targets named: **-0.5 and -0.618** [2:04–2:09], with "the negative 618 level is particularly powerful in extended uptrends" [2:16–2:21].
- Setup/context required: An established trend — for the long example, an **uptrend defined as a series of higher highs and higher lows** [1:16–1:22]. Then a **pullback after a fresh higher high**, forming a higher low [2:22–2:30].
- Entry trigger: Two conditions, both required:
  1. Price retraces into the **38.2%–61.8% band** and starts moving back up [0:53–1:02, 1:36–1:38].
  2. Price then **breaks the previous higher high WITH an increase in volume**, and you **enter at the candle close above the higher high** [1:38–1:46]. "we don't enter the trade just because the price touches a Fibonacci level, we need further confirmation" [1:09–1:16]. He is emphatic: "this is why you should always wait for the breakout" [4:34–4:39].
- Stop loss: **"just below the recent higher low, or slightly below the 618 Fibonacci retracement level"** [1:44–1:52]. In the worked example: "our stop loss is placed below the recent higher low" [2:49–2:52].
- Take profit: **Fibonacci extension -0.5 or -0.618** [2:02–2:09]; worked example used **-0.618** [2:52–2:59]. Stated R on his live trades: **1:4** [3:13–3:16, 4:00–4:03].
- Filters he adds: **Volume spike must confirm the breakout** ("we see a strong volume spike confirming the breakout" [2:42–2:45]) — magnitude never quantified. **Risk-reduction after losses:** after two losers he cut size — "I only risk a half a percent of $1,000 on this" [3:54–4:00], vs "my original 1% risk trade" [4:27–4:29]. **Bias-flip rule:** "because my stop-loss should be the confirmation that the trend is reversing against my initial bias, I immediately entered in a short position" [3:27–3:38] — same flip-on-stop-out logic as AlsXNhTm4AA.
