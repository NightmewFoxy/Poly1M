# Best Scalping Entry - Price action

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
Explicitly a follow-up to his earlier three-line-strike video; here he adds the 21 MA and a trade-viability filter, and contrasts scalps with longer-term double-bottom trades.

- Indicators + exact settings: **21-period moving average** [1:41] — "just stick up a 21 period moving average, the 21 is a good short term trend". **Type (SMA/EMA/SMMA) is not stated in this video** (his TMA overlay elsewhere uses smoothed). Alternative offered: draw a trend line "in any way that you want" [1:35].
- Setup/context required:
  - A clearly trending market. Downtrend = lower highs + lower lows; uptrend the reverse [4:53].
  - Price trading **below the 21 MA** (for shorts) and **rejecting off the 21** [1:46] — "almost every single time that it rejected off of the 21 it was a three-line strike".
  - Scalp **with the trend only** [5:39].
- Entry trigger: **a three-line strike at the 21 MA rejection — three candles against the trend then one engulfing candle with the trend — and the engulfing candle must be genuinely engulfing.** [2:40] "all of these rejected but this is the only one that had an engulfing candle where this candle is completely bigger than this candle... once you find that engulfing candle that's going to be your entry point." Confirmed on the 5-minute chart after being spotted at the 1-hour rejection zone.
- Stop loss: **just above the swing high** (for a short) — "really really tight" [2:54]; mirrored below the swing low for longs.
- Take profit: three options he names at [2:58–3:07]: the **previous swing low**, or a **1:2 risk-to-reward**, or **20 pips**.
- Filters he adds — the key mechanical filter of this video, a **trade-viability check before entering**:
  - [3:08] "if the trade does not fall within your parameters — if that swing low is not more than 20 pips you don't take the trade; if you don't get a one to two risk to reward ratio on that swing low you don't take the trade."
  - [3:41] a 1:1 is explicitly not worth risking 1%: "if you're only getting a one to one it's not really worth risking one percent if your target is that close". Also skip when the stop is bigger than the distance to the target [3:52].
  - Stop taking trend continuations once structure breaks [4:04] "you can't just keep taking every single one of these three-line strike trades ... because you can see you're making lower lows and lower highs then it makes a higher high breaking the trend."

Separate (non-scalp) setup taught in the same video — **double-bottom reversal on the 1-hour**:
- Enter on the **rejection off the double bottom** [4:43].
- Stop loss: "a nice comfortable distance below this double bottom just in case it makes a triple" [4:52] — no number given.
- Take profit: "where the price crossed a lot on this swing high", or previously marked support/resistance [5:01].
- Example result: **7 days held, 113 points** on US30 [5:10].

General framing [0:05]: long-term trades need wider stops and far targets; scalps need "the most exact surgical entry, very small stop loss and a quick take profit".
