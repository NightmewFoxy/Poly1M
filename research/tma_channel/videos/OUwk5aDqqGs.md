## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
The video's actual subject is a **chart-colour / psychology change**, but it does
narrate one complete worked trade, so both are logged.

- Indicators + exact settings: none. Only manually drawn support and resistance
  levels.
- Setup/context required ([1:46]-[2:12]): two known support/resistance levels
  already marked from prior price action. Price drops to the lower level, rejects,
  and rejects a **second** time off the same level — **a double bottom (a "W") on
  a support and resistance line**. He calls this "really healthy, it's a good
  entry" and credits his separate support-and-resistance videos.
- Entry trigger ([2:18]-[2:32]): after **the second rejection candle off the
  support line has CLOSED**, enter long. Explicitly: "you've had your second
  rejection off of that support and resistance line, **the candle has closed**, and
  you are ready for a long position."
- Stop loss ([2:32]-[2:36]): **below the support line.** In the worked example this
  was a **15 pip stop loss** ([4:19]-[4:24]).
- Take profit ([2:36]-[2:40], [4:19]-[4:24]): **the previous resistance level** —
  "target that whole move back up to the previous resistance." In the example that
  was a **73 pip take profit**, i.e. roughly **1:4.9 R** ("that's a banger trade").
- Anti-rule he calls out ([3:18]-[3:36]): **do not take a late entry** after price
  has already moved away from the level — "the reason this is wrong is because now
  you don't have a concrete area for your stop loss and you should have been down
  here but now you're just randomly placing it anywhere that you feel comfortable."
- Filters he adds: none (no session, no day, no news filter in this video).

## The actual "trick" — chart configuration (fully mechanical, non-price)
- [5:04]-[5:22] **Change the long/short position-tool colours** away from
  green/red. His suggestion: **blue and white on a black background.** In
  TradingView: click the position tool → colour options → change the *profit
  background colour* and the *stop background colour*.
- [5:41]-[6:09] **Change the candle/bar colours to neutral colours.** Right-click
  chart → Settings → bar colour; replace all greens and all reds. He picks
  **yellow and white**.
- [7:20]-[7:41] The assignment: "for the next week ... completely change your candle
  colours, even make it all white whether it's going up or down ... as long as they
  are **neutral** and they don't entice certain emotions like stop and go."
- Rationale ([1:13]-[1:34], [2:40]-[3:18]): red/green are culturally conditioned as
  stop/go, so an open long sitting in a red drawdown zone triggers a panic exit
  before the analysis has had time to play out.
- [6:38]-[7:20] He notes MT4 mobile defaults to white/black candles, says he does
  "99% of my trading on my phone" for exactly this emotional neutrality.
