# Forex Trading Secret **Stop Hunting**
- id: K3TuPYfCJMM | views: 143000 | length: 431s
- market(s) shown: US30 (~1 month of price action)
- timeframe(s) taught: 1h (and 4h) for the markup; explicitly warns 1m/5m scalpers to keep these levels in mind

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: **none** — pure horizontal support/resistance markup, no indicator is added at any point.
- Setup/context required: mark **major** support and resistance on the **1-hour (or 4-hour)** chart by drawing horizontal lines across the tops and bottoms of moves — "these areas right here where the price just abruptly stops is a major level of support and resistance" [1:33-1:43]. Explicitly: do this on a higher timeframe because lower-timeframe levels are not major [1:11-1:24]. Minor consolidation levels get a thinner line or a different colour so you know they are secondary [1:52-2:09].
- Entry trigger: **do not** take the breakout. The rule is defensive first — treat the empty space just beyond a major level ("where there's literally nothing living up here") as a **liquidity pool** and avoid breakout entries into it [2:37-2:50, 4:10-4:26]. The offensive version: wait for the bank move to reveal itself — after the sweep, once you see big momentum candles moving away from the liquidity pool, join that move ("you can get on board with the bank and follow their trade") [5:28-5:46].
- Stop loss: not stated. He notes the failure mode is traders having **no** stop loss and getting margin-called [0:56-1:00, 4:00-4:10], but gives no placement rule.
- Take profit: hold "as long as you feel comfortable with, **or until you see that next liquidity pool**" [5:46-5:50]. Worked example: a 600-point US30 move.
- Filters he adds: exercise extreme caution on **any** breakout scenario [4:24-4:26]; do the 1h/4h liquidity markup as the first thing you do when you get on the charts [5:19-5:23]; recognise the sweep pattern — break of level, then a wicky range that gives longs false hope, then big opposite candles [3:41-4:00].
