# How to Technical Analysis
- id: 5rN2bQXB4Vo | views: 152000 | length: 510s
- market(s) shown: NAS100
- timeframe(s) taught: **1-hour for markup/bias**, then **15-minute** for the scalp; 1-minute mentioned as the beginner mistake

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings:
  - **TTF SMC Toolkit** — draws the **market open price**, the **midnight line**, and each session's **first hour** = the "**DR / IDR range**" [4:36–4:56].
  - **Fibonacci retracement** — drawn **bottom → top** of the leg; he looks for a retrace into the "**Fibonacci gold zone**" (no numeric level stated in this video) [2:49–2:54].
  - **Trend lines** drawn at **45 degrees** [2:29–2:40].
  - **Happy Trail** (paid TTF buy/sell signal indicator), **"case 3"** preset — gives on-chart signals plus a **1-hour higher-timeframe confluence** [6:34–6:43].
- Setup/context required (his stated workflow):
  1. Zoom out to the **1-hour**, fitting several weeks/months of price (his example spans end-of-April to today) [1:44–2:01].
  2. Mark the **pivot low** and **pivot high**, then put a **horizontal ray on every pivot point where price retraced a little then shot up aggressively — "you want these V shapes"** [2:01–2:18]. These are the liquidity levels price must clear.
  3. Draw **45-degree trend lines**; a trend line with **multiple touches** that then breaks (after a failure to make a higher high) sets the bias — here **bearish** [2:29–2:44].
  4. Fib the leg: two scenarios — price runs straight down to the far liquidity level, or it retraces into the **gold zone** first; he calls the gold-zone retrace "the safe bet" [2:44–3:03]. Exact quoted levels: price at **14,009.39**, most likely target **14,022**; deeper swing-trade target **12,713** [2:56–3:13].
  5. Drop to the **15-minute**: note the short-term counter-trend (uptrend inside an hourly downtrend) and identify the S/R zone ("support and resistance or supply and demand or order block whatever you want to call it they're all the same") [3:13–3:39].
  6. Expect a **liquidity sweep**: price wicks above the zone/trend line to grab liquidity, then rejects [3:53–4:14].
- Entry trigger: at the marked zone, take the **Happy Trail signal candle** — "**take a long position at close of candle**" [7:10–7:11]. Direction is filtered by short-term bias: "we don't take the bearish signal because our short-term bias is bullish up to this trend" [7:06–7:10].
- Stop loss: **not stated** in this video (never placed on the example trade).
- Take profit: **the marked zone / next drawn blue line** — "Target that zone one to two point three" i.e. **1:2.3 R** [7:11–7:14]; and for the session-break play "once it broke that zone you could have simply targeted the next Blue Line that we had drawn up" [5:16–5:20].
- Filters he adds:
  - **Never trade the first opening hour of any session** — "one thing to keep in mind we do not trade the first opening hour of any session it is an absolute No-No" [6:54–7:00].
  - **DR/IDR session rule**: once price breaks **above or below** the first-hour range of a session, "it will usually trade that for the remainder of the day" [4:56–5:07].
  - **Tokyo session usually stays relatively flat** (low volume) [5:32–5:38].
  - **Set price alerts and walk away** — the video's headline discipline rule. He marks the level (**alert on NAS100 at 14,954.83**, "crosses that point ... only once"), then leaves the charts and waits for the phone alert instead of watching [7:14–8:05].
  - Time window stated for the day's plan: "**between now and 9 A.M. we are looking for price to come up to our trend line and reject below our blue support and resistance and take a short trade for today**" [6:11–6:21] — **timezone unstated**.
