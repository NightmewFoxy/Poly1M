# Day Trading Stop Loss: Maximize Profits, Minimize Risk

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
This video's whole payload is a **stop-loss placement rule**, not a full strategy.

- Indicators + exact settings: **ATR Bands** (TradingView, "ATR bands by The Trade Floor", free) with the **ATR band scale factor set to 1.5** — "I find it best to set your ATR bands at 1.5 meaning one is the actual average true range and 1.5 is 1.5 times the average true range" ([4:11]). The ATR *length* is never stated (left at whatever the indicator defaults to).
- Setup/context required: Trending 1-minute price action making higher highs and higher lows (or the inverse). He draws a **trend line** across the swings; **when price breaks that trend line he starts trading the new trend** ([0:51]–[0:56]).
- Entry trigger: a **price-action rejection candle formation at the trend line**, entered on that candle. Two named by name: the **evening doji star** — "a big candle up, a doji, and a candle down" ([1:20]–[1:28]) — and the **dragonfly doji** for longs ([4:44]). Also fires on a simple new higher-high/higher-low rejection: "you can use this every single time that price rejects a trend line or just simply makes a higher high and a higher low" ([4:34]).
- Stop loss: **1.5 × ATR from entry**, read straight off the ATR Bands, instead of the conventional "just above/below the signal wick". The rule he is replacing: "the normal way that people do it is stop loss just above the price with a one to two risk to reward — that doesn't work all the time; sometimes price comes back up smacks your stop loss and then goes in that direction" ([1:33]).
- Take profit: **none stated in this video.** He references a **1:2 RR** as the conventional pairing he is critiquing ([1:35]) and a **1:3 RR** as the default preset in the paid risk calculator ([3:30]) — neither is presented as this video's rule.
- Filters he adds: trades **pre-market open** in a short session ("I can get multiple trades in my small trading session pre-market open, but if you have free time you can trade all day", [0:45]). Position sizing: **risk 1% of account balance** per trade ([3:26]). Invalidation rule: if price goes 1.5×ATR against you, **you were wrong** — "if price goes against that 1.5 times the average true range it's most likely going to go the opposite direction meaning that you are wrong about your entry" ([5:01]).
