# How to Find The Best Entry for Your Trade
- id: igc4dgeyLw8 | views: 310000 | length: 611s
- market(s) shown: US30
- timeframe(s) taught: **1 hour** for the markup and all five examples ("what I want you to do is do [the analysis] on the one hour time frame") [2:31-2:36]

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings:
  - Three **smoothed moving averages: 21, 50, 200** — specifically the TradingView script "**three smooth moving average by Hamid Safi**" [1:10-1:22]
  - **RSI**: remove the 30 line and the 70 line, **set both to 50**; long only above 50, short only below 50; "we will never be looking at overbought and oversold" [1:24-1:48]
  - Third slot, pick one: the "**engulfing candle indicator by rmunoz/bremunos**" if you can't spot engulfing candles by eye [1:51-2:10], **or** the **Stochastic RSI** if you can. Stoch RSI settings: on **15m and higher leave the defaults 3, 3, 14, 14**; **below 15m change the stochastic length to 8** [2:14-2:31].
  - For the analysis pass he tells you to **hide all indicators** and mark up price naked first [2:34-2:46].
- Setup/context required: on the 1h, mark every peak and every multiple-touch support/resistance zone; "you don't want to look too far back because that price action is no longer relevant" [2:46-3:11]. Then five entry types, usable alone or stacked [3:14-3:33].
- Entry trigger (the five entries):
  1. **Divergence** — price makes a lower low while RSI makes a higher low, at a support zone → long on the rejection [3:36-4:33]
  2. **Rejection with an engulfing candle / three-line strike** — a momentum move down then a big engulfing candle, "preferably eating the previous three candles" [4:42-5:16]
  3. **Support/resistance zone trading** — enter at a zone and target the next zone [5:29-6:14]
  4. **Double tops / double bottoms** (listed as one of the five at [3:23], but never demonstrated in the video)
  5. **Break and retest** — price breaks above a zone, comes back hard and rejects off it with a big bullish candle → long [6:56-7:23]
  - Cross-check on every entry: "make sure you check the RSI and know that it's above the 50 level when going in on a long position; if it is below the 50 level and you're looking for short positions you are also good to go" [7:39-7:47]
- Stop loss: "obviously your stop loss is going to be below the previous low right here" [6:43-6:48]. No other placement rule given.
- Take profit: **the next support/resistance zone** [5:44-6:06, 7:23-7:26], and where MAs are involved, "you can easily target the next subsequent moving average, which is also a key level of support and resistance" [8:26-8:33]. Risk-reward: "keeping this ratio at about **one to two**" [6:48-6:52]. Worked figures: 180 points held for 3 hourly candles [5:16-5:26]; a 240-point US30 target to the next zone [6:34-6:43].
- Filters he adds: RSI-50 side filter on every entry (above); MA slope/direction as the reason price rejects — "as the moving averages are all swooping up we are in a clear uptrend" [7:51-8:01]; **double confluence** — rejection off a solid S/R zone *and* off the 21 SMMA in an uptrend is called the highest-quality version [8:01-8:16]; only look at recent price action, not far back [3:05-3:11].
