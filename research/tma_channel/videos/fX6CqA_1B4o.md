- Setup/context required (top-down, stated as a sequence):
  1. On the DAILY, "focus on the last swing high and swing low ... wherever the price rejected, put a horizontal ray" [0:56-1:04].
  2. Drop to the HOURLY to fine-tune those levels [1:12-1:17].
  3. Identify the flip: a level that was resistance, broke, and is now expected to act as support (or vice versa) [1:20-1:45, 3:22-3:28].
  4. Drop to the entry timeframe and wait for price to return to that zone: "we are looking for price action to reject off of this zone because it used to be resistance and now we're anticipating it to be support" [2:18-2:26].
- Entry trigger: a three-line strike printing INSIDE the marked S/R flip zone, in the direction of the higher-timeframe bias — "get in on a three line strike there in the direction of your bias" [3:46-3:51]. "price rejected with a three line strike ... long position here" [2:37-2:45].
- Stop loss: explicitly NOT at the wick — "do not put your stop loss at the end of the wick because that's only seven pips. Give yourself some breathing room: 15, 17 pips" [2:45-2:54]. **15-17 pips.**
- Take profit: "just target the previous high, that is almost guaranteed. Anything after that basically you should be trailing" [2:54-3:00].
- Filters he adds:
