## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
This is the **multi-timeframe target upgrade** to his 5-minute scalping strategy — it explicitly answers "what do you do when the 5m and the higher timeframe contradict" and points back to the original 5m scalping video at [5:41].

- Indicators + exact settings: **21, 50 and 200 period moving averages** ("I like to use the 21 period moving average the 50 and the 200", [1:10]) — MA *type* never stated in this video. Plus **RSI** for divergence — length and levels never stated.
- Setup/context required (the reversal sequence he describes, [1:31]–[1:52], all on 5m):
  1. Price makes **higher highs while RSI makes lower highs** = bearish (regular) divergence.
  2. Price then **cascades down through the moving averages** (21 → 50 → 200).
  3. Price **breaks below the 200 MA**, comes back up to **retest it**, and **rejects it**.
  4. That confirms a downtrend on the 5-minute.
- Entry trigger: **short on the close of the candle that rejects the 200 MA** — "you can either enter in on this candle after it closed once it rejected the 200 or if you want to be safe and secure you could have done this huge red candle" ([1:57]). In the worked trade he enters "after these two large rejection candles of that 200 period moving average" ([3:40]).
- Stop loss: **at the 200 period moving average on the 5-minute time frame** ([3:47]).
- Take profit: **the 200 period moving average of the 15-minute (higher) time frame** — this is the video's core rule. "when doing this multi-time frame analysis your price target your profit target for your trade should always be relatively close to the 200 period moving average" ([2:47]) of the higher timeframe. That combination produced **1:2 risk-to-reward** in his example ([3:58]).
- Filters he adds:
  - **Timeframe pairing must be adjacent**: "if you're trading the five minute you want to check the 15 minute, if you're trading the 15 minute you want to check the one hour, if you're trading the one hour you want to check the four hour or the daily" ([2:14]).
  - **Trend definition = position vs the 200 MA**: above 200 = uptrend, below = downtrend ([2:42]).
  - Divergence should be **confirmed on both timeframes** (5m and 15m) ([2:36], [4:00]).
  - He flags the trade as **counter-trend on the 15m** and warns "counter trend trading is dangerous" ([3:10]) — which is precisely why the target is capped at the higher-TF 200 MA.
  - Priority rule: "the rule of trading with the trend still remains priority number one — you are trading with the trend on the time frame that you're trading… but you are also aware of the overall trend on the higher time frame" ([4:54]).
