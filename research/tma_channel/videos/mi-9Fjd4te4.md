# Market Structure Explained – Stop Trading Blind
- id: mi-9Fjd4te4 | views: 45000 | length: 345s
- market(s) shown: BTCUSD (5-minute)
- timeframe(s) taught: 5-minute in the live example; **general rule: confirm structure across two timeframes — 15m for entries, 1h for confirmation**

## Mechanical rules (only what the video actually states)
- Indicators + exact settings: none required. He plugs his paid "**happy trail**" indicator at [5:01] as an entry-candle aid, with no settings given.
- Setup/context required (his own stated cheat sheet, [0:44]–[0:58]):
  1. Identify the recent swing high and swing low.
  2. Zoom out to see the trend direction.
  3. Wait for a **break of structure (BOS)** or a **change of character (CHoCH)**.
  4. Only trade in the direction of the current structure.
  5. "Don't enter during the transition. Wait for the shift to complete, then follow the structure."
- Definitions given (mechanical):
  - **Uptrend** = each high higher than the previous high AND each low higher than the previous low ([1:21]).
  - **Downtrend** = each low lower than the previous low AND each high lower than the previous high ([1:32]).
  - **BOS** = in an uptrend, breaking through a prior higher high; in a downtrend, breaking through a prior lower low ([1:54]–[2:10]).
  - **CHoCH** = the counter-trend break that ends the run — in the BTC example, price **broke above the most recent lower high** while in a downtrend ([4:03]–[4:14]).
- Entry trigger: **the marking rule is the closest thing to a trigger and it IS specific** — "every single one of your lower lows, I want you to draw a circle and a dotted line. **The dotted line must be across the bottom of the body of the candle. Do not include the wicks with this structure.** Once price breaks through it fully and **closes the candle below that line**, then you can signify it as a break of structure" ([3:31]–[3:52]). Filter: "**try to ignore these single candle pullbacks as they are insignificant**" ([3:52]).
- Stop loss: **none stated.**
- Take profit: **none stated.**
- Filters he adds:
  - **Two-timeframe confirmation, explicitly numbered**: "always confirm structure across two time frames. For example, 15-minute structure for entries and 1 hour structure for confirmation" ([3:10]).
  - Only trade with the trend ("the trend is your friend", [5:26]).
  - Four named mistakes to avoid ([2:38]): trading against structure; entering on a weak pullback; confusing CHoCH with BOS; ignoring higher timeframes (he calls the last "the most critical").
  - After a CHoCH, wants to **see the volume increase** before entering ([5:14]).
